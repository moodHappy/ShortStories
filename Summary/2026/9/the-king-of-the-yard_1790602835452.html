The King of the Yard

发布时间：2026/9/28 21:40:28

院子里的王

Arthur Thorne leaned back against the coarse concrete wall of the federal correctional facility’s recreation yard, letting the harsh autumn sunlight wash over his face.

📌 Key Expressions

  • lean back against sth. = 向后靠在某物上

  • coarse concrete wall = 粗糙的混凝土墙；coarse = 粗糙的、质地不细腻的

  • federal correctional facility = 联邦惩教机构；通常指联邦监狱/拘留设施

  • correctional facility = 惩教设施；比 prison 更正式、官方

  • recreation yard = 监狱里的活动场地/放风场

  • the recreation yard of a correctional facility = 惩教设施的活动场

  • harsh autumn sunlight = 刺眼、强烈的秋日阳光；harsh = 刺眼的、强烈而令人不适的

  • let sth. wash over sb. = 任由某物笼罩/洒遍某人

  • let the sunlight wash over his face = 任由阳光洒在脸上

  • wash over = （光线、气味、情绪等）笼罩、席卷

  • leaning back..., letting... = 向后靠着……，任由……；两个 -ing 分词结构表示伴随动作

  • the federal correctional facility's recreation yard = 联邦惩教设施的活动场地；facility's 是所有格

  • facilitys → 应为 facility's，因为这里表示“该惩教设施的活动场地”。

亚瑟·索恩靠在联邦惩教所粗糙的混凝土操场墙壁上，任由刺眼的秋日阳光洒在脸上。

At forty-two, Arthur wasn't built like the traditional enforcers who ruled the cell blocks through brute force alone. He was serving a twenty-year sentence for white-collar securities fraud and high-level wire theft—crimes that required a sharp mind, absolute coldness, and an innate understanding of how leverage worked.

📌 Key Expressions

  • at forty-two = 四十二岁时；这里 at + 年龄 表示“在……岁时”

  • be built like sb. = 体格长得像某种类型的人；built 在这里指体格、身材

  • traditional enforcers = 传统意义上的打手/强制执行者；enforcer = 靠暴力执行命令、维持权威的人

  • rule the cell blocks = 控制监狱牢房区/牢房楼层；rule = 统治、控制

  • cell block = 监狱牢房区；由许多牢房组成的区域

  • through brute force = 通过蛮力；依靠纯粹的暴力

  • brute force alone = 单靠蛮力；alone = 仅仅、单独依靠

  • He wasn't built like... = 他的体格并不像……；not ... like ... 用于否定相似性

  • who ruled the cell blocks... = 控制牢房区的那些人；who 引导定语从句，修饰 enforcers

  • serve a sentence = 服刑；sentence = 刑期

  • a twenty-year sentence = 二十年刑期

  • serve a twenty-year sentence for sth. = 因某罪被判二十年刑期

  • white-collar crime = 白领犯罪；通常指金融、商业、职务等领域的非暴力犯罪

  • securities fraud = 证券欺诈

  • wire theft = 电信/网络盗窃；这里是犯罪语境中的具体罪名表达

  • high-level = 高级别的；这里强调犯罪行为的层级、复杂程度或规模

  • high-level wire theft = 高级别/复杂的电信盗窃

  • crime that requires sth. = 需要某种能力/特质的犯罪

  • require a sharp mind = 需要敏锐的头脑

  • sharp mind = 敏锐、聪明的头脑

  • absolute coldness = 极度冷酷；absolute 在这里起强调作用

  • an innate understanding of sth. = 对某事天生的/与生俱来的理解能力

  • innate = 天生的；与生俱来的

  • an understanding of how sth. works = 对某事如何运作的理解

  • how leverage worked = 杠杆/制衡手段如何发挥作用；how + 从句 作 understanding 的内容

  • leverage = 杠杆；引申为影响力、制衡力、谈判筹码

  • how leverage worked = 如何利用优势、筹码或制衡关系施加影响

  • who ruled... = 定语从句

  • crimes that required... = 定语从句；that 修饰 crimes

  • a sharp mind, absolute coldness, and an innate understanding... = 三项并列结构，用来列举完成这类犯罪所需的能力

四十二岁的亚瑟并不像那些仅凭野蛮暴力统治牢房的传统打手。他因白领证券诈骗和高级电汇窃盗正服刑二十年——这些罪行需要敏锐的头脑、绝对的冷酷，以及对杠杆原理的本能理解。

In here, money didn't talk the way it used to, but information, commissary items, and protection currency were the universal language. Arthur spoke it fluently.

在这里，金钱不再像从前那样万能，但情报、小卖部物资和保护伞货币才是硬通货。亚瑟对这一切驾轻就熟。

The sudden scuffle by the weight benches caught his eye. A fresh batch of transferred inmates had just been herded into the yard, looking pale, hollow-eyed, and terrified.

重量训练区那边突然传来的骚动吸引了他的注意。新一批转监的犯人刚刚被驱赶进操场，他们脸色苍白、眼神空洞、惊恐万状。

Among them, a familiar sycophantic buzzing sound drew Arthur’s attention. It was Marcus.

在人群中，一个熟悉的、透着谄媚劲儿的声音吸引了亚瑟的注意。那是马库斯。

Even inside a maximum-security prison block, Marcus hadn't dropped his old habits. Within forty-eight hours of arrival, the younger inmate had already attached himself to Arthur’s operation like a tick, managing to secure the softest work-detail assignment in the prison laundry and smuggling the best contraband coffee straight to Arthur’s bunk.

即便在最高安全级别的监狱里，马库斯也改不了老毛病。入狱不到四十八小时，这个年轻犯人就像个牛皮糖一样贴上了亚瑟的势力范围，不仅设法弄到了监狱洗衣房里最轻松的差事，还把最好的违禁咖啡偷运到了亚瑟的铺位上。

"I'm telling you, Arthur, the supply chain for the electronic workshop tablets is completely open if we just tweak the routing software," Marcus murmured eagerly, scurrying over with a fresh cup of hot brew in a chipped ceramic mug.

“我跟你说，亚瑟，只要我们稍微调整一下路由软件，电子工作坊平板电脑的供应链完全可以为我们所用，”马库斯殷勤地小跑过来，用一个破损的马克杯递上一杯新鲜热腾腾的咖啡。

Marcus had quickly realized that survival here meant buying protection, and he was an expert at finding the apex predator to serve.

马库斯早就明白，在这里生存就意味着买通保护伞，而他极其擅长寻找食物链顶端的掠食者并为之效劳。

Arthur took a slow sip, his sharp eyes evaluating the nervous, eager-to-please kid standing before him.

亚瑟慢条斯理地抿了一口，锐利的目光审视着眼前这个紧张、急于讨好的年轻人。

Marcus was useful. He handled the digital bookkeeping for the block’s contraband pool, fetched extra portions from the mess hall, and massaged Arthur’s bruised ego with a constant stream of calculated flattery and deference.

马库斯很有用。他负责处理整个监区违禁品池的数字账目，从食堂弄来加量伙食，并用源源不断的精明恭维和顺从抚慰着亚瑟高傲的自尊心。

In exchange, Marcus slept on a bottom bunk away from the violent drafting currents of the main cell block, enjoying a surprisingly comfortable, insulated existence behind bars.

作为交换，马库斯睡在远离主监区暴力漩涡的下铺，在铁窗后过着出乎意料舒适且受到庇护的日子。

Arthur let out a low, dry chuckle, tossing a piece of contraband candy across the concrete table.

亚瑟发出了一声低沉而干瘪的轻笑，将一颗违禁糖果隔着混凝土桌子扔了过去。

"Keep your eyes on the ledger, Marcus," Arthur said smoothly, his voice carrying the calm authority of a man who owned the room without lifting a finger. "And make sure that coffee is hot tomorrow morning. We have business to run."

“管好你的账本，马库斯，”亚瑟语气平稳地说道，声音里透着一个无需动手便能掌控全局之人的平静威严。“还有，明天早上确保咖啡是热的。我们还有生意要谈。”
