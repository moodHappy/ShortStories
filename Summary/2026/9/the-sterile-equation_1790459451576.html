The Sterile Equation

发布时间：2026/9/27 06:08:36

无菌方程式

At 8:15 PM, the ice in his sparkling water had completely melted. Julian sat in the corner booth of Le Petit Hibou, watching the screen of his phone. The message remained unanswered.

📌 Key Expressions

  • at 8:15 PM = 晚上8:15；具体时间点前用 at
  • the ice in his sparkling water = 他气泡水里的冰块
  • sparkling water = 气泡水
  • completely melted = 完全融化了；melt = 融化
  • the ice had completely melted = 冰块已经完全融化；过去完成时，强调在某个过去时间点之前已经发生
  • sit in the corner booth = 坐在角落的卡座里
  • corner booth = 角落里的卡座
  • watch the screen of his phone = 看着手机屏幕
  • the message remained unanswered = 那条消息一直没有得到回复
  • remain + adjective / past participle = 仍然处于某种状态
  • remain unanswered = 一直未被回复；unanswered = 未得到回答/回复
  • The message remained unanswered. = 消息发出去后一直没有得到回应；这里 remain 强调“状态持续未改变”

晚上8点15分，他气泡水里的冰块已经完全融化了。朱利安坐在“小猫头鹰”酒馆的角落卡座里，注视着手机屏幕。那条信息依然没有回复。

He didn't feel rejection; he felt a mild, clinical annoyance, like a disrupted algorithm. The city outside was boiling in a heatwave, but inside the restaurant, the air was heavily filtered and smelled of expensive truffles and chilled wine.

他没有感到被拒绝；他只感到一种轻微的、临床般的烦躁，就像一段被打乱的算法。外面的城市正在酷暑中沸腾，但餐厅里的空气经过了重重过滤，弥漫着昂贵的松露和冰镇葡萄酒的气味。

He placed a crisp fifty-dollar bill on the flawlessly ironed tablecloth and stood up. Claire was an erratic, unpredictable variable. He despised unpredictability.

他在熨烫得毫无褶皱的桌布上放下一张崭新的五十美元钞票，站起身来。克莱尔是一个古怪且不可预测的变量。他厌恶不可预测。

He walked two blocks to the Obsidian Hotel, his tailored suit completely immune to the oppressive humidity. Suite 402 was pre-paid.

他步行两个街区来到黑曜石酒店，他那剪裁得体的西装对压抑的潮湿天气完全免疫。402套房是提前付过款的。

He had meticulously engineered this evening: the polite dinner, the calculated conversation, the inevitable transition to this sterile, king-sized bed. Now, the schedule was empty, a void that irritated him deeply.

他一丝不苟地设计了今晚：文雅的晚餐，经过计算的对话，以及必然转移到这张无菌特大号床上的结局。现在，时间表空了，这种空虚感让他深感恼火。

Entering the room, he adjusted the thermostat to a precise sixty-five degrees. The silence was absolute, a stark contrast to the chaotic, grinding hum of the streets below.

走进房间，他将恒温器调到了精确的华氏六十五度。这里有着绝对的寂静，与楼下街道上混乱、刺耳的嗡嗡声形成了鲜明的对比。

He needed a substitute to balance the evening's equation. He poured a glass of neat scotch, opened his encrypted contact list, and made a brief, discreet phone call to an elite escort agency.

他需要一个替代品来平衡今晚的方程式。他倒了一杯纯威士忌，打开加密的通讯录，给一家高级应召女郎机构打了一个简短而隐秘的电话。

Forty minutes later, the door chimed. The woman who entered was impeccably groomed. Her name was Sylvia—at least, that was the name on her escort profile. She smelled of synthetic jasmine and expensive cosmetics, her smile practiced and entirely empty.

四十分钟后，门铃响了。走进来的女人打扮得无可挑剔。她叫西尔维娅——至少在她的高级应召资料上是这个名字。她身上带着合成茉莉花和昂贵化妆品的气味，笑容训练有素且完全空洞。

"Mr. Vance?" she asked softly, stepping into the foyer.

“万斯先生？”她轻声问道，迈进门厅。

Julian didn't offer her a drink. He locked the deadbolt with a sharp click. Walking past her, he unfastened his cufflinks, tossing them onto the glass table. He didn't care about who she was, only what he had paid for.

朱利安没有给她倒酒。他伴随着“咔哒”一声脆响锁上了门栓。他从她身边走过，解开袖扣扔在玻璃桌上。他不在乎她到底是谁，只在乎他付钱买来的服务。

He turned around and grabbed a fistful of Sylvia's perfectly styled hair. With a cold, mechanical grip, he pulled her face abruptly down toward his crotch.

他转过身，一把抓住西尔维娅做过完美造型的头发。带着一种冰冷、机械的力道，他粗暴地将她的脸直接按向自己的胯下。

"We have this room until midnight," he said, his voice flat and devoid of any human warmth. "Let's skip the pleasantries and get to work."

“这个房间我们能用到午夜，”他声音平淡，不带一丝人情味地说。“我们跳过客套，直接开始工作吧。”
