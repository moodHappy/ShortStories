Economic Downturn Hits Preschools: Kindergarten Teachers Are Losing Their Jobs Too

发布时间：https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/wfI5JtIjUSuQ14wgCG8dPA

经济下行冲击幼教行业：幼儿园老师也开始失业了

In September, while taking driving lessons, I unexpectedly ran into Ms. Yuan, one of my daughter’s former preschool teachers. She was learning to drive too.

9月份去驾校练车时，我意外遇到了女儿以前的一位幼儿园老师——袁老师。她当时也在学车。

“Ms. Yuan, are you not working these days?” I asked, surprised. “How come you have time to take driving lessons?”

我有些惊讶地问她：“袁老师，你最近没上班吗？怎么有时间来学车了？”

She replied calmly, “No, I’m not working anymore. I’ve left the kindergarten.”

她淡淡地回答：“嗯，我没上班了，不在幼儿园干了。”

I was a little surprised and asked her what had happened. She told me that there were fewer and fewer children, and with enrollment falling, the kindergarten no longer needed as many teachers. On top of that, the pay had never been particularly good, so she had decided to leave.

我有些惊讶，便问她缘由。她告诉我，现在孩子越来越少，幼儿园生源缩水，园里不需要那么多老师，再加上工资本来就不高，索性就离开了。

She also told me that Ms. Li, her former co-teacher and my daughter’s other teacher, had already left the job as well.

她还告诉我，和她搭班的李老师，也就是我女儿的另一位老师，也已经离职了。

My daughter attended a private kindergarten for her first year, before we transferred her to a public kindergarten for the second year.

我女儿小班是在私立幼儿园，中班我们就转到了公立幼儿园。

Ms. Yuan was employed on a contract basis, earning around 2,500 yuan a month, with five types of social insurance and a housing provident fund.

袁老师是合同聘用制，月薪大概2500块，缴纳五险一金。

From the outside, being a teacher who looks after young children may seem like a respectable job. But only those who have actually done it know how exhausting it can be.

这份工作外人看着体面，是带小朋友的老师，只有身在其中才知道有多熬人。

Teachers have to arrive early every morning and look after dozens of children as they eat and nap. They must constantly keep an eye on the children's safety, deal with crying and conflicts, complete paperwork and prepare for all kinds of inspections. The work is repetitive, demanding and mentally draining.

每天早早到园，照顾几十个孩子吃喝午睡，要时刻盯着安全，处理哭闹、矛盾，还要写资料、应付各类检查，事情琐碎又耗费心力。

During this summer's enrollment period, the number of children dropped sharply. The kindergarten reduced its classes from five to three. With fewer classes, fewer teachers were naturally needed, and rumours of staff cuts began quietly circulating around the kindergarten.

今年暑假招生时，生源锐减，原先5个班压缩成3个班。班级少了，老师自然就要精简，裁员的消息在园里悄悄传开。

Rather than wait to be laid off, Ms. Yuan decided to take the initiative. She thought it over: the salary was low, the work was exhausting, and there was little sign of improvement in the industry's overall prospects. It might be better to resign voluntarily and start planning for the future.

与其等着被裁，被动离开，袁老师想了想：工资不高、工作很累，行业大环境又看不到好转，不如主动辞职，早点为以后做打算。

She submitted her resignation during the summer holidays and stopped going to work in September.

暑假她就递交了辞职信，9月便不再去上班。

She had always been busy with work during the week, while her weekends were taken up by family responsibilities. It was only after leaving her job and gaining some free time that she was able to start taking driving lessons in September.

她以前一直上班，周末又被家庭填满，正是因为离职了，有了时间，9月才来学车考驾照。

Most people think of teaching as a stable profession. After all, new children enter kindergartens every year, so how could there possibly be a shortage of pupils?

大家印象里，老师是稳定的职业，尤其是幼儿园，年年都有小朋友入园，怎么会缺生源？

I used to think that unemployment was something far removed from the early childhood education sector. But the reality is right in front of us: the ripple effects of declining birth rates are already being felt by people working in the industry.

以前我总觉得，失业离幼教行业很远。可现实摆在眼前，生育率下降带来的连锁反应，已经实实在在落到幼教从业者身上。

Private kindergartens are bearing the brunt. Without guaranteed government funding to fall back on, they see their revenue shrink when enrollment falls short. Staffing costs are among the first expenses to be cut.

私立园首当其冲，没有财政兜底，招生一旦跟不上，收入缩水，最先压缩的就是人力成本。

For a monthly salary of just over 2,000 yuan, teachers shoulder enormous responsibilities. Now, even these jobs are no longer secure.

两千多的工资，扛着巨大的责任，现在连这份工作都不再稳当了。

Many preschool teachers studied early childhood education, obtained teaching certificates and entered the profession full of enthusiasm. In the end, however, they are being pushed out by forces beyond their control.

很多幼师读了学前教育专业，考了教师资格证，一腔热情投入进去，最后却被大环境推着离开。

And it is not just these two teachers. I have come across accounts from many parts of the country of small private kindergartens closing down or merging, leaving large numbers of teachers with little choice but to change careers. Some have gone into sales, others have found jobs in supermarkets, while some, like Ms. Yuan, are using this unexpected break to learn new skills and explore other career options.

不止这两位老师。我刷到不少地方的小型民办园关停、合并，大批幼师被迫转行。有的去做销售，有的去超市上班，还有的像袁老师一样，趁着空档学新技能，重新摸索出路。

When the tides of change sweep through society, ordinary people often have little control over their own lives. We tend to assume that certain professions will always exist, overlooking the long-term impact of demographic changes.

时代浪潮袭来，普通人都身不由己。我们总以为有些职业永远不会消失，却忽略了人口变化带来的长远影响。

Fewer children mean fewer kindergarten places and fewer teaching jobs. Preschool teaching, once regarded as a relatively stable profession filled with warmth and care, is now facing growing pressure simply to survive.

孩子少了，幼儿园的岗位跟着变少，曾经被视作温柔安稳的幼师岗位，如今也开始面临生存压力。

Watching Ms. Yuan concentrating on her driving lessons, I couldn't help feeling emotional. It wasn't that she no longer liked children; it was simply that life had to go on, and she needed to find a new path for herself.

看着驾校里认真练车的袁老师，心里挺感慨。她不是不喜欢孩子，只是生活要继续，人总要为自己寻找新出路。

In the face of broader economic challenges, no job is absolutely secure. For ordinary people, all we can do is keep learning new skills and give ourselves more options for the future.

大环境之下，没有谁的工作是绝对铁饭碗。普通人能做的，就是多学一点本事，给自己多留一条后路。
