Liu Yandong, 81, Dies

发布时间：2026/10/28 21:18:34

刘延东逝世，享年81岁

BEIJING, Oct. 28, 2026 — Before Liu Yandong became one of China’s most senior female officials, her working life began far from the corridors of power. After graduating from Tsinghua University, she went to work in a chemical factory in Tangshan, Hebei province, rising from worker to technician and eventually taking charge of a polysilicon workshop. More than four decades later, she would be serving as one of China’s vice-premiers.

北京，2026年10月28日——在成为中国级别最高的女性官员之一之前，刘延东的职业生涯始于远离权力中心的工厂车间。清华大学毕业后，她前往河北唐山的一家化工厂工作，从工人做起，逐步成为技术员，后来还担任多晶硅车间负责人。四十多年后，她走进了中国国务院领导层，出任副总理。

Liu died in Beijing on Wednesday afternoon at the age of 81, according to an official announcement carried by Xinhua News Agency. Her career spanned factory work, youth organisations, united-front affairs and the highest levels of central government. It was a journey through several different worlds of China’s political system, rather than a rise confined to a single policy field.

据新华社发布的官方消息，刘延东于周三下午在北京逝世，享年81岁。从工厂工作到青年组织，从统战事务到中央政府高层，她的职业生涯横跨中国政治体系中的多个领域，而非局限于某一项专业工作。

Her early years were shaped by an unusual combination of technical training and political responsibility. Liu entered Tsinghua University’s engineering chemistry department in 1964. While studying, she also served as a political counsellor for her year group. After graduating in 1970, she began working in industry, first in Tangshan and then at a chemical plant in Beijing.

她的早年经历有一个颇为特别的组合：一边接受技术教育，一边承担政治工作。1964年，刘延东进入清华大学工程化学系学习，同时担任年级政治辅导员。1970年毕业后，她进入工业部门工作，先后在唐山和北京的化工厂任职。

The factory years did not last forever. In 1980, Liu moved into the Beijing municipal party apparatus. Two years later, she joined the leadership of the Communist Youth League, beginning a long period in youth affairs and public organisations. The move marked a turning point: her career was no longer centred on industrial production but increasingly on political coordination and administration.

工厂生涯并非她职业道路的终点。1980年，刘延东进入北京市委组织部门工作；两年后，她进入共青团中央领导层，开始长期从事青年事务和社会组织工作。这是她职业生涯的重要转折点：她的工作重心不再是工业生产，而是逐渐转向政治协调与行政管理。

In 1991, Liu moved to the Communist Party’s United Front Work Department, later becoming its head in 2002. The department works to build and maintain relationships between the party and groups beyond its core membership, including people in business, intellectual and other social circles. It was a different kind of political work from running a factory, requiring sustained contact with people and organisations across a wide range of backgrounds.

1991年，刘延东转入中共中央统一战线工作部，并于2002年出任部长。该部门负责加强和维持中国共产党与党内核心成员以外群体之间的联系，包括工商界、知识界及其他社会领域人士。这与管理工厂是截然不同的工作，需要长期与背景各异的个人和组织打交道。

Her rise continued in stages. Liu entered the Communist Party’s Politburo in 2007, became a state councilor in 2008 and was appointed vice-premier in 2013. She remained in the latter post until 2018. She was among a small number of women to reach the upper levels of China’s political leadership and was the fourth woman to serve as vice-premier.

此后，刘延东的仕途继续稳步推进。2007年，她进入中共中央政治局；2008年，出任国务委员；2013年，升任国务院副总理，并一直任职至2018年。她是中国少数进入高层领导岗位的女性之一，也是中国历史上第四位女性副总理。

Yet her public responsibilities were not primarily associated with the economic or security portfolios that often dominate political headlines. As vice-premier, Liu was closely linked to education, science and technology, healthcare, culture and sport. These were fields with direct consequences for universities, hospitals, research institutions and ordinary families, even when the work itself attracted less attention than high-profile political confrontations.

不过，她的公共职责并不主要集中在新闻头条中常见的经济或安全事务上。担任副总理期间，刘延东的工作与教育、科技、医疗卫生、文化和体育等领域密切相关。这些事务直接影响高校、医院、科研机构和普通家庭，只是相关工作往往不像高调的政治交锋那样引人注目。

Her career also reflected the importance of political networks and institutional experience in China’s system. Years in the Communist Youth League were followed by senior responsibilities in united-front work and then central government administration. Rather than moving directly from a technical profession into national leadership, Liu passed through a series of organisations that gave her experience in personnel work, political liaison and government coordination.

她的经历也体现了政治网络与组织历练在中国政治体系中的重要性。从共青团到统战部门，再到中央政府，她一步步承担更高级别的职责。刘延东并非从技术岗位直接进入国家领导层，而是先后经过多个组织体系，在干部工作、政治联络和政府协调等方面积累经验。

After leaving office in 2018, Liu largely disappeared from the daily political spotlight. The years that followed were markedly quieter than the decades she had spent moving through the party and state hierarchy. Her death now closes a career that began in a chemical factory and ended among the country’s most senior political offices.

2018年卸任后，刘延东逐渐淡出日常政治新闻的焦点。与此前数十年在党和国家领导体系中不断晋升的经历相比，她退休后的生活显得平静许多。如今，她的离世为这段从化工厂起步、最终走入国家最高层级领导岗位的职业生涯画上了句号。

The official announcement did not disclose further details about her illness or funeral arrangements. Her public record, however, traces an unusual professional arc: from engineering chemistry and factory management to youth affairs, united-front work and the State Council, across more than four decades of service.

官方消息没有进一步披露她所患疾病的具体情况，也未公布葬礼安排。但她留下的公开履历勾勒出一条颇为独特的职业轨迹：从工程化学与工厂管理，到青年事务、统战工作，再到国务院领导岗位，跨越四十余年的职业生涯。
