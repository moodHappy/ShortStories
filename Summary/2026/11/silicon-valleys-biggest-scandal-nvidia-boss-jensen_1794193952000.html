Silicon Valley’s Biggest Scandal? Nvidia Boss Jensen Huang Admits Infidelity as Spy Allegations Rock the Tech World

发布时间：2026/11/9 11:12:32

硅谷年度大瓜？英伟达掌门人黄仁勋承认婚外情，神秘女高管又牵出间谍疑云

Forget AI chips for a moment. The hottest topic in Silicon Valley this week isn't Nvidia's latest technology — it's the very personal drama surrounding its billionaire boss, Jensen Huang. The man who helped turn Nvidia into an AI powerhouse is now making headlines for a reason that has absolutely nothing to do with semiconductors.

先把AI芯片放一边吧。这周硅谷最热门的话题，不是英伟达又推出了什么新技术，而是这家科技巨头的亿万富豪掌门人黄仁勋的私人生活。曾经带领英伟达成为AI产业霸主的男人，如今登上新闻头条的原因，却和半导体一点关系都没有。

The CEO Says It Himself: “I Was Unfaithful to My Wife”

CEO亲口承认：我对妻子不忠

On November 8, Huang sent a message to Nvidia employees admitting that he had been unfaithful to his wife. He apologized to his wife and children, saying there was no excuse for his actions and that he took full responsibility for the hurt he had caused. He also said he hoped his family could heal and that he might eventually earn back their trust.

11月8日，黄仁勋向英伟达员工发送了一封信，承认自己对妻子不忠。他向妻子和孩子道歉，表示自己的行为没有任何借口，并愿意为造成的伤害承担全部责任。他还说，希望家人能够慢慢走出这场伤害，也希望有一天能够重新赢回他们的信任。

That is quite an admission from one of the world's most powerful business leaders. Huang did not name the woman in his message, but the timing has put an earlier media investigation back under the spotlight.

对于全球最有影响力的商业领袖之一来说，这无疑是一番相当直接的认错。黄仁勋并没有在信中透露那名女性的名字，但这封信出现的时间，让此前媒体披露的一桩调查再次成为焦点。

Enter Alice Huang: Business Executive, Mystery Woman — and Alleged Intelligence Asset?

神秘女主角登场：商业高管，还是疑似情报人员？

The woman at the centre of the reports is Alice Huang, an executive associated with Megaspeed, a Singapore-based company that buys Nvidia chips. According to media reports citing US intelligence concerns, American officials suspected that she had links to Chinese intelligence and may have been involved in efforts to obtain sensitive information or influence access to advanced Nvidia chips.

报道中被推到风口浪尖的女性名叫 Alice Huang。她曾在新加坡公司 Megaspeed 担任高管，而这家公司是英伟达芯片的买家。根据媒体援引美国情报部门相关担忧所作的报道，美国方面怀疑她与中国情报机构存在联系，并可能参与获取敏感信息或影响英伟达先进芯片对华供应的活动。

The New York Times and other outlets have reported that Huang had an intimate relationship with Alice Huang. Jensen Huang's own message confirms that he was unfaithful to his wife, but it does not identify the woman involved. The espionage allegations, meanwhile, remain allegations — not proven facts.

《纽约时报》等媒体报道称，黄仁勋与 Alice Huang 存在亲密关系。黄仁勋本人承认了婚外情，但没有在公开信中点名这名女性。至于她是否从事间谍活动，目前仍属于指控和调查方向，并非已经证实的事实。

So the story has two very different layers: an affair that Huang has acknowledged, and a much more serious intelligence question that investigators still need to examine. One is a personal scandal; the other could become a national-security headache.

所以，这件事其实有两个完全不同的层面：一段黄仁勋已经承认的婚外情，以及一个仍有待调查的重大情报问题。前者是私人丑闻，后者则可能演变成国家安全层面的麻烦。

From Private Romance to a Boardroom Investigation

从私人关系闹到董事会：这瓜已经不只是家庭纠纷

Nvidia's board is not treating the matter as ordinary celebrity gossip. It has announced an independent investigation into reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. The company says it currently has no reason to believe that Nvidia secrets or national security information were compromised, but the inquiry will examine all available information.

英伟达董事会显然没把这件事当成普通名人八卦。董事会已经宣布，将针对黄仁勋可能成为外国情报行动目标的相关报道展开独立调查。公司表示，目前没有理由认为英伟达的商业机密或国家安全信息已经遭到泄露，但调查将审查所有可获得的信息。

And here's another twist: Huang is not stepping down. Nvidia says he will continue serving as CEO while the investigation proceeds. In other words, the company is trying to keep its AI empire running while its boss deals with a scandal that has suddenly become much bigger than his marriage.

还有一个值得注意的细节：黄仁勋并没有辞职。英伟达表示，在调查进行期间，他将继续担任CEO。换句话说，公司一边要维持庞大的AI商业帝国正常运转，一边还得处理这场突然超出婚姻纠纷范围的风波。

A Billionaire, a Long Marriage and a Very Public Apology

亿万富豪、数十年婚姻，以及一封不得不公开的道歉信

Huang and his wife, Lori, have been married for decades and have two children. For years, the Nvidia founder has been widely recognised for his black leather jacket, energetic public appearances and almost rock-star status in the technology industry. Now, the headlines are focusing on a far less polished side of his life.

黄仁勋与妻子 Lori 已经结婚数十年，两人育有两个孩子。多年来，这位英伟达创始人一直以标志性的黑色皮衣、充满激情的公开演讲，以及近乎摇滚明星般的科技圈地位为人熟知。如今，媒体关注的却是他生活中完全不同的一面。

His apology was addressed not only to his family but also to Nvidia's employees, board members, partners and shareholders. He acknowledged that his personal conduct had fallen short of the standards expected of him as the company's leader. That's a remarkable sentence when your private life has suddenly become a corporate governance issue.

他的道歉对象不仅包括家人，还包括英伟达员工、董事会成员、合作伙伴和股东。他承认，自己的私人行为没有达到公司领导者应有的标准。当一个人的私生活突然变成公司治理问题时，这句话显得格外耐人寻味。

For gossip lovers, the obvious questions remain: How long did the relationship last? How did the two meet? And what exactly did Nvidia's board know before the story exploded? Those details have not all been publicly established, so for now, the internet will have to resist filling in the blanks with imagination.

对于吃瓜群众来说，几个问题显然还悬而未决：这段关系持续了多久？两人究竟是怎么认识的？在事情曝光之前，英伟达董事会又掌握了多少情况？这些细节目前并没有全部公开，网上的各路“侦探”暂时还不能靠想象把剧情补完整。

One thing, however, is already clear: this is no longer just a story about a billionaire's marriage. With Nvidia's board investigating possible foreign intelligence involvement, the scandal has moved from the family home to the corporate boardroom — and potentially into the arena of national security.

但有一点已经很清楚：这不再只是一位亿万富豪的婚姻故事。随着英伟达董事会调查可能涉及的外国情报活动，这场风波已经从家庭内部闹到了公司董事会，甚至可能进一步进入国家安全领域。

The AI king may be used to making headlines with his chips. This time, it's his personal life doing all the talking.

这位AI芯片之王或许早已习惯靠芯片登上头条，但这一次，替他抢尽风头的却是自己的私人生活。
