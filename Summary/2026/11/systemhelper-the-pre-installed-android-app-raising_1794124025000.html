SystemHelper: The Pre-installed Android App Raising Privacy Concerns Across China

发布时间：2026/11/8 15:47:05

SystemHelper：一款引发中国安卓用户隐私担忧的预装应用

BEIJING, Nov. 8, 2026 — A little-known system application buried deep inside some Chinese-market Android phones has become the focus of an expanding privacy debate. Known as SystemHelper, the app has drawn scrutiny from developers and security researchers after reverse-engineering reports suggested that it can collect extensive device information, including identifiers linked to mobile subscribers, location-related data and records of app usage.

北京，2026年11月8日——一款隐藏在部分中国大陆版安卓手机系统深处、鲜为普通用户所知的应用，近日成为一场隐私争议的焦点。这款名为 SystemHelper 的应用引起了开发者和安全研究人员的关注。多份逆向分析报告显示，它具备收集大量设备信息的能力，其中包括与手机用户身份相关联的设备及SIM卡标识、位置相关数据，以及应用使用记录。

The package, identified as com.mobiletools.systemhelper, was initially brought to public attention through an examination of a Xiaomi 14 running China's HyperOS 3. The investigation subsequently prompted users to look for the same package on devices from other manufacturers, including OPPO, vivo, realme and other brands. Reports of similar components have since emerged across different Android platforms, although the exact packages, versions and functions are not necessarily identical.

该应用的包名为 com.mobiletools.systemhelper。最初，一名开发者在检查一部运行澎湃OS 3的国行小米14时发现了这一组件，随后相关调查引发用户对其他品牌手机的排查，包括 OPPO、vivo、realme 等。此后，不同安卓平台上也陆续出现了有关类似组件的审计报告。不过，各品牌设备中的具体软件包、版本和功能并不一定完全相同，不能简单地将某一款手机上的发现套用到所有品牌。

A Carrier Service Hidden in the System

隐藏在系统深处的运营商服务组件

According to publicly available reverse-engineering reports, SystemHelper appears to function as a carrier device-management or registration client. On the Xiaomi device examined by one developer, the application was found in a privileged system directory and contained a configured reporting endpoint associated with China Unicom: dm.wo.com.cn:18080.

根据目前公开的逆向分析报告，SystemHelper 的功能似乎与运营商设备管理或终端注册服务有关。在一名开发者检查的小米设备上，该应用被发现安装在系统特权目录中，其配置中包含一个与中国联通相关的上报地址：dm.wo.com.cn:18080。

One particularly controversial finding was that the component reportedly continued attempting to register with the Unicom platform even when the phone was using a SIM card from China Mobile. This raised questions about why a carrier-specific service would communicate with Unicom's infrastructure regardless of which operator's SIM card was installed.

其中一个引发争议的发现是：即使手机插入的是中国移动的SIM卡，该组件据称仍会尝试向联通平台注册。这引发了一个值得追问的问题：如果这是一项与特定运营商有关的服务，为什么在用户使用其他运营商SIM卡时，它仍然会与联通的服务器通信？

The available evidence points to a component associated with carrier terminal management, rather than an ordinary application installed by the phone's owner. Its presence in privileged system partitions also means that it can receive permissions unavailable to most regular apps. However, the precise contractual or regulatory requirements behind its inclusion have not been publicly established, and neither its presence nor its technical capabilities alone prove that every manufacturer deploys it under identical arrangements.

现有证据指向的是一款与运营商终端管理有关的系统组件，而不是手机用户自行安装的普通应用。由于它被放置在系统特权分区中，因此能够获得大多数普通应用无法取得的权限。不过，目前尚无公开材料充分说明其预装背后的具体合同或监管要求。仅凭它出现在系统中，也不能证明所有手机厂商都是按照完全相同的安排部署这一组件。

What Can It Collect?

它能够收集哪些信息？

The most significant concern lies in the range of information that the application appears capable of accessing. According to published code analyses and device audits, the data categories include several types of information that, when combined, could provide a detailed picture of a device and its user's activities.

最受关注的问题在于，该应用似乎能够访问的信息范围相当广泛。根据已经公开的代码分析和设备审计结果，其涉及的数据类别包括多种信息。如果这些信息被结合起来，便可能勾勒出一部设备及其使用者活动情况的详细画像。

The first category consists of device and subscriber identifiers. These may include the IMEI, which identifies the handset, as well as the IMSI and ICCID, which are associated with the mobile subscription and SIM card. A phone number may also be included where available. Such identifiers can make it possible to associate technical records with a particular device or mobile subscription.

第一类是设备和用户身份标识。其中可能包括用于识别手机设备的 IMEI，以及与移动通信用户身份和SIM卡相关联的 IMSI、ICCID。在能够获取的情况下，手机号码也可能包含在内。这类标识符可能使技术记录与某一具体设备或移动通信账户建立关联。

A second category involves network and location information. The reports describe access to cellular network details such as CellID and LAC, signal-strength measurements and location coordinates obtained through location services, including Baidu's location SDK. These records could potentially reveal information about a device's approximate whereabouts and network environment.

第二类涉及网络和位置信息。相关报告提到，该组件能够访问 CellID、LAC 等蜂窝网络信息、信号强度数据，以及通过定位服务获取的经纬度信息，其中包括百度定位SDK。此类记录在一定条件下可能反映设备的大致位置及其所处的网络环境。

A third category concerns application activity. The analyses describe the ability to enumerate installed applications and retrieve usage statistics, including which apps were used during a recent period, how long they remained in the foreground and how much network traffic they consumed. This is particularly sensitive because an app list and usage history can reveal far more than a device's technical specifications: they may offer clues about a person's interests, habits, work patterns and digital activities.

第三类则涉及应用使用情况。相关分析显示，该组件具备枚举已安装应用并读取使用统计信息的能力，包括用户近期使用过哪些应用、各应用在前台运行了多长时间，以及消耗了多少网络流量。这一点尤其敏感，因为应用清单和使用记录所透露的信息远远超出设备本身的技术参数，还可能反映一个人的兴趣、习惯、工作规律及数字生活轨迹。

Some reports describe roughly 40 categories or fields of collected information. That figure should not, however, be interpreted as proof that every field is collected and successfully transmitted on every device. The permissions granted to an application, the functions implemented in its code, the records stored locally and the information actually sent to a remote server are four different things.

部分报告将其涉及的信息概括为约40项或40类字段。不过，这个数字不能被理解为每部手机都会采集并成功上传所有这些信息。应用获得的权限、代码中实现的功能、本地保存的数据，以及最终发送到远程服务器的信息，是四个不同层面的概念，不能混为一谈。

Encryption Does Not Eliminate the Privacy Question

加密并不能消除隐私争议

The network transmission method has also attracted attention. Reverse-engineering reports describe data being submitted through HTTP POST requests, while the payload itself is encrypted using AES. In other words, the communication does not necessarily expose the underlying information as readable plain text, but the connection reportedly lacks TLS protection at the transport layer.

数据传输方式同样引发了关注。逆向分析报告称，该组件通过 HTTP POST 请求提交数据，同时使用 AES 对载荷本身进行加密。换句话说，传输内容并不一定以可直接阅读的明文形式暴露，但据报告，其通信在传输层缺少 TLS 保护。

The distinction matters. AES encryption can make the contents of a captured request difficult to read without the relevant key. However, using HTTP rather than HTTPS leaves aspects of the connection without the protections normally provided by TLS, including authenticated server identity and protection against certain forms of interception or tampering. Payload encryption and secure transport are complementary safeguards, not interchangeable ones.

这一差别十分重要。如果没有相应密钥，AES加密可以使截获的请求内容难以被直接读取。但与HTTPS相比，使用HTTP意味着连接缺少TLS通常提供的一些保护，例如对服务器身份的认证，以及对某些拦截和篡改行为的防护。载荷加密与安全传输是相互补充的两种保护措施，并不能彼此替代。

The use of HTTP does not, by itself, prove that the data has been intercepted or misused. Equally, the presence of AES encryption does not answer the more fundamental questions: who controls the receiving infrastructure, what information is retained, how long it is stored, who can access it and for what purposes it is processed.

当然，使用HTTP本身并不能证明数据已经遭到拦截或滥用。同样，采用AES加密也无法回答更根本的问题：接收服务器由谁控制？数据会被保存哪些内容？保存多长时间？哪些主体能够访问？这些信息最终又会被用于什么目的？

A Privileged App That Ordinary Users May Not Be Able to Remove

普通用户难以卸载的特权应用

SystemHelper's installation method adds another layer to the controversy. On examined devices, the application was found in directories such as /system/priv-app/ or /product/priv-app/. Unlike a conventional app downloaded from an app store, a component installed in these locations may be treated by Android as a privileged system application.

SystemHelper 的安装方式进一步加剧了争议。在已检查的设备上，该应用被发现位于 `/system/priv-app/` 或 `/product/priv-app/` 等目录中。与用户从应用商店下载的普通应用不同，安装在这些位置的组件可能被安卓系统视为特权系统应用。

Reported permissions include READ_PRIVILEGED_PHONE_STATE, access to background location, QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES and PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS. These permissions can provide access to sensitive device identifiers, location-related information, installed-app inventories and usage statistics, subject to the operating system's permission checks and implementation.

报告中列出的权限包括 READ_PRIVILEGED_PHONE_STATE、后台定位权限、QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES 和 PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS。这些权限可能使应用访问敏感设备标识、位置相关信息、已安装应用清单及使用统计数据，但实际能否访问以及访问范围，仍取决于操作系统的权限检查和具体实现。

On some models, users have reported that the Force Stop and Uninstall controls are unavailable or grayed out in system settings. This is not necessarily evidence of malicious behavior: privileged system components are often protected from ordinary removal because manufacturers or carriers consider them part of the device's software infrastructure. Nevertheless, the lack of a clear user-facing explanation or straightforward opt-out mechanism makes meaningful user control more difficult.

在部分机型上，用户反映系统设置中的“强行停止”和“卸载”按钮无法使用，或呈现灰色状态。这并不必然意味着应用存在恶意行为：厂商或运营商往往会将特权系统组件视为设备软件基础设施的一部分，因此限制普通用户将其删除。尽管如此，如果缺少清晰的用户说明和便捷的退出机制，用户就很难真正掌控这些组件的运行。

Not Every Privacy Component Is SystemHelper

并非所有隐私相关组件都是 SystemHelper

As the discussion spread, SystemHelper was frequently confused with Xiaomi's own system security and anti-fraud components. The distinction is important because different applications may have different developers, destinations, permissions and functions.

随着讨论不断扩散，SystemHelper 经常被与小米自有的系统安全及反诈组件混为一谈。这一区分十分重要，因为不同应用可能拥有不同的开发主体、数据接收地址、权限和功能。

Some public analyses have described screen-capture or anti-fraud detection mechanisms associated with Xiaomi's security software. Those findings should not automatically be attributed to SystemHelper. The available SystemHelper investigations focus primarily on device registration, identifiers, network and location information, and app-usage data. They do not establish that SystemHelper itself routinely records screens or listens to conversations.

一些公开分析确实提到，小米安全软件中存在与屏幕采集或反诈检测有关的机制。但这些发现不能自动归到 SystemHelper 名下。目前有关 SystemHelper 的调查主要集中在设备注册、设备标识、网络与位置信息以及应用使用数据等方面，并没有证据证明 SystemHelper 本身会常规录制屏幕或监听对话。

The wider lesson is that a phone may contain several independent system-level services with overlapping access to sensitive information. Treating them as one single surveillance application can lead to inaccurate claims, while examining each package, its code and its actual network behavior separately provides a more reliable picture.

更值得注意的是，一部手机中可能存在多个彼此独立的系统级服务，而它们对敏感信息的访问权限可能有所重叠。如果把这些组件统统归为同一个监控应用，容易产生不准确的说法。分别检查每个软件包的代码、权限和实际网络行为，才能更可靠地了解问题。

The Evidence Is Growing, but Important Questions Remain

证据不断增加，但仍有关键问题待解

Further investigations published in early November have added device-specific evidence. One audit of a Xiaomi Redmi running Android 16 confirmed the presence of SystemHelper in a privileged system directory and examined its permissions and code. However, during a short observation period, the researchers did not detect new network traffic from the component. This illustrates why static code analysis and live network observation must be considered separately.

11月初公布的进一步调查，为这一事件增加了针对具体设备的证据。一份针对运行 Android 16 的小米 Redmi 手机的审计报告，确认 SystemHelper 存在于系统特权目录中，并检查了它的权限和代码。不过，在一段较短的观察时间内，研究人员没有检测到该组件产生新的网络流量。这也说明，静态代码分析与实时网络监测必须分开看待。

A separate analysis of a realme GT5 Pro also reported local records and app-usage-related data associated with the component. Such findings strengthen the case that the issue is not necessarily limited to a single Xiaomi model. But they still do not establish that every device from every named manufacturer contains the same version or behaves in exactly the same way.

另一份针对 realme GT5 Pro 的分析也报告称，在设备本地发现了与该组件有关的记录和应用使用数据。这些发现使人们有理由怀疑，相关问题未必只存在于某一款小米手机上。但它们仍不足以证明所有被提及的手机厂商、所有机型都安装了相同版本的组件，或具有完全一致的行为。

There is also an important distinction between SystemHelper and other registration services. A November 6 investigation of a OnePlus device, for example, did not find the SystemHelper package. Instead, it identified a separate component, com.coloros.regservice, with its own local records and reporting code. The researcher said that actual successful transmission of the records and the identity of the receiving party had not been conclusively verified. This is a reminder that similar privacy concerns do not necessarily point to the same software or the same operator.

此外，还必须区分 SystemHelper 与其他设备注册服务。例如，11月6日公布的一份一加手机调查，并未发现 SystemHelper 这一软件包，而是识别出另一个独立组件：`com.coloros.regservice`。研究人员在该组件中发现了本地数据记录和上报代码，但表示尚未确凿证实这些记录是否已经成功传出，也未确认接收方的具体身份。这提醒我们：不同手机上出现相似的隐私问题，并不意味着它们一定来自同一款软件或同一个运营主体。

The central unanswered questions remain substantial. Do the collected records leave the device in every case? How frequently are they transmitted? Which organization ultimately receives them? What is the legal basis for collecting and processing the information? Are users clearly informed, and can they refuse without losing essential phone functions? Public reverse-engineering reports have not yet provided complete answers to all of these questions.

目前仍有许多核心问题没有得到回答：这些采集记录是否在所有情况下都会离开手机？数据多久传输一次？最终由哪个机构接收？收集和处理这些信息的法律依据是什么？用户是否得到了清晰告知？他们能否在不影响手机基本功能的情况下拒绝此类数据收集？现有公开逆向分析报告尚未对这些问题给出完整答案。

A Broader Debate Over Consent and Transparency

关于用户同意与软件透明度的更大争论

The controversy goes beyond one package name. Modern smartphones depend on a complex ecosystem involving manufacturers, mobile operators, chipset vendors, app developers and third-party service providers. System-level components may be installed to support device registration, network diagnostics, anti-fraud services or other operational requirements. Some of these functions may serve legitimate purposes, but their presence does not remove the need for clear disclosure and proportionate data collection.

这场争议已经超出了一个软件包名称本身。现代智能手机依赖一个复杂的生态系统，其中涉及手机厂商、移动运营商、芯片平台供应商、应用开发者以及第三方服务提供商。系统级组件可能用于设备注册、网络诊断、反诈服务或其他运行需求。这些功能可能具有合理用途，但其存在并不意味着厂商可以免除清晰告知用户和遵循适度数据收集原则的责任。

The issue is particularly sensitive when information such as app usage, location and subscriber identifiers is combined. Each data category may appear relatively ordinary in isolation. Together, however, they can potentially reveal patterns of behavior and link digital activity to a specific device or subscription. That makes transparency, data minimization, retention limits and independent security audits especially important.

当应用使用记录、位置信息和用户身份标识被结合起来时，问题就显得格外敏感。单独来看，每一类数据似乎都很普通；但如果将它们整合起来，就可能揭示用户的行为模式，并将数字活动与某一部具体设备或通信账户联系起来。因此，信息透明度、数据最小化、保存期限限制以及独立安全审计都显得尤为重要。

For users who discover the package on their phones, developers have circulated ADB commands that can remove or disable the application for the current Android user without modifying the system partition. However, this approach does not physically remove the pre-installed APK from the system image, and the component may return after a factory reset or system update. Removing a system package can also affect carrier-related functions, so blindly copying commands intended for another model is not advisable.

对于在手机中发现这一软件包的用户，开发者和社区已经分享了一些 ADB 命令，可以在不修改系统分区的情况下，为当前安卓用户卸载或禁用该应用。不过，这种操作并不会真正删除系统镜像中的预装 APK；恢复出厂设置或系统更新后，该组件仍可能重新出现。此外，删除系统软件包也可能影响与运营商有关的功能，因此不建议用户不加判断地照搬针对其他机型的命令。

Ultimately, the SystemHelper controversy highlights a question that applies to the entire smartphone industry: when a system component can access information about a user's device, location and app activity, who should decide what is collected and where it goes? Technical functionality may explain why a component exists, but only transparency, verifiable safeguards and meaningful user choice can establish whether that functionality is being used appropriately.

归根结底，SystemHelper 事件提出了一个适用于整个智能手机行业的问题：当一个系统组件能够访问用户的设备信息、位置和应用活动记录时，究竟应该由谁决定收集什么数据，以及这些数据最终流向哪里？技术功能或许能够解释一个组件为什么存在，但只有透明的信息披露、可验证的安全保障和真正有效的用户选择权，才能让人判断这些功能是否得到了恰当使用。

For now, the available evidence supports serious questions about the scope of data collection and the transparency of pre-installed carrier services. It does not, by itself, establish that every affected phone is being continuously monitored, that all collected information is successfully transmitted, or that the application is malicious. The next step should be independent verification and clear responses from the relevant manufacturers and operators.

目前，已有证据足以让人严肃审视预装运营商服务的数据收集范围及其透明度问题。但仅凭这些材料，还不能断定所有涉及的手机都在持续受到监控，也不能认定所有被采集的信息都已成功上传，更不能直接将该应用定性为恶意软件。接下来，更需要的是独立验证，以及相关手机厂商和运营商给出清晰、具体的回应。
