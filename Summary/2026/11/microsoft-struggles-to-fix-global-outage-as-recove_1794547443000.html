Microsoft Struggles to Fix Global Outage as Recovery Efforts Fail to Go as Expected

发布时间：2026/11/13 13:24:03

微软全球服务大规模宕机，修复工作未达预期

November 13, 2026 | Technology

微软 11 月 13 日表示，针对全球性服务故障采取的修复措施未能达到预期效果。此次故障影响了 Outlook、Microsoft Teams、Copilot 以及其他 Microsoft 365 云服务。公司正在寻找替代方案，以恢复受影响的服务器和服务。

Microsoft said on November 13 that its efforts to fix a global service outage were not going as expected. The disruption has affected Outlook, Microsoft Teams, Copilot and other Microsoft 365 cloud services. The company is now exploring alternative solutions to restore affected servers and services.

1. A Global Disruption

全球范围的服务中断

The disruption began at around 18:00 GMT on Thursday, November 12, initially affecting Microsoft's Exchange Online email service. Users in dozens of countries and territories reported problems accessing their inboxes, sending and receiving emails, and connecting to workplace applications.

此次故障始于 11 月 12 日星期四格林尼治时间约 18:00，最初影响微软的 Exchange Online 邮件服务。数十个国家和地区的用户报告称，他们无法正常访问收件箱、收发电子邮件，或连接办公应用程序。

According to Downdetector, an outage-tracking website, reports of problems with Outlook peaked at nearly 130,000 in the United States alone. Other affected services included Teams, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, SharePoint, OneDrive and Microsoft's AI assistant Copilot.

根据故障追踪网站 Downdetector 的数据，仅美国地区的 Outlook 故障报告峰值就接近 13 万条。其他受到影响的服务还包括 Teams、Word、Excel、PowerPoint、SharePoint、OneDrive，以及微软的人工智能助手 Copilot。

The disruption also affected Microsoft 365 connectors used by AI companies OpenAI and Anthropic, while businesses and government agencies in several countries reported difficulties communicating and accessing their systems.

此次故障还影响了 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 使用的 Microsoft 365 连接器。多个国家的企业和政府部门也报告称，通信和系统访问受到影响。

2. What Went Wrong?

故障究竟出在哪里？

Microsoft initially said that part of its infrastructure was experiencing elevated loads, contributing to connectivity problems. However, the company later clarified that the problem was not caused by an overall increase in user traffic.

微软最初表示，其部分基础设施负载过高，导致连接问题。不过，公司随后澄清，问题并非由整体用户流量增加造成。

Instead, a number of servers had unexpectedly become unavailable after failing automated health checks. As a result, traffic was redirected to the remaining operational servers, putting additional pressure on an already weakened infrastructure.

实际上，部分服务器未能通过自动健康检查，因而意外停止提供服务。结果，大量流量被转移到其他仍在运行的服务器上，进一步加重了剩余基础设施的负担。

Microsoft's investigation pointed to a specific component responsible for processing server health information. The company also found that a core process on affected servers was becoming overwhelmed, causing them to stop responding to requests.

微软的调查将问题指向一个负责处理服务器健康状态信息的特定组件。公司还发现，受影响服务器上的一个核心进程出现过载，导致服务器无法继续响应请求。

3. Why Have Microsoft's Fixes Failed?

为什么微软的修复措施没有奏效？

Microsoft initially attempted to restart affected servers and introduce an alternative method for checking their health. The company reported that this approach had successfully brought a subset of servers back online.

微软最初尝试重启受影响的服务器，并采用一种替代性的服务器健康检查方法。公司曾表示，这一方案已经成功让部分服务器重新上线。

However, the recovery did not proceed as smoothly as hoped. Some servers that initially appeared healthy became unresponsive again once they started receiving traffic.

然而，恢复工作并没有想象中顺利。一些最初看起来运行正常的服务器，在重新接收流量后再次失去响应。

In a subsequent update, Microsoft acknowledged that the changes it had implemented to improve service availability were not working as expected.

在随后的一次更新中，微软承认，为改善服务可用性而采取的调整措施并未取得预期效果。

"We've monitored the status of the changes applied to improve serviceability and confirmed that change is not taking as expected."

“我们已经监测了为改善服务可用性而实施的调整措施，并确认这些调整并未按照预期发挥作用。”

The company said it was identifying alternative approaches to increase capacity and address persistent imbalances in traffic management.

微软表示，公司正在寻找其他解决途径，以提高系统容量，并解决持续存在的流量管理失衡问题。

4. Is It a Cyberattack?

这是否是一场网络攻击？

Microsoft has said it does not believe the incident is related to a cyberattack. Australian officials also indicated that the company did not believe it was dealing with a cyber event.

微软表示，公司认为此次事件并非网络攻击。澳大利亚官员也透露，微软方面认为这次故障不属于网络安全攻击事件。

The company has not publicly established a definitive root cause. Its investigation has focused on server health-check failures, an overwhelmed core process and the resulting loss of infrastructure capacity.

微软尚未公开确认最终的根本原因。目前，其调查重点集中在服务器健康检查失败、核心进程过载，以及由此造成的基础设施容量损失。

5. Government Agencies and Businesses Affected

政府部门和企业受到波及

The outage has disrupted government operations in several countries. Australian federal and state government departments reported problems with email and other digital services.

此次故障扰乱了多个国家政府部门的正常运作。澳大利亚联邦及州政府部门报告称，电子邮件和其他数字服务受到影响。

Japan also reported disruptions to government IT infrastructure. In the United States, the House of Representatives was among the organizations affected.

日本方面也报告了政府信息技术基础设施受到影响。在美国，众议院也是受到此次故障波及的机构之一。

For businesses that depend heavily on Microsoft's cloud-based services, the disruption has created difficulties in everyday operations, particularly email communication and workplace collaboration.

对于高度依赖微软云服务的企业而言，此次中断给日常运营带来了困难，尤其影响了电子邮件通信和办公协作。

6. What Happens Next?

接下来会发生什么？

Microsoft is continuing to investigate the underlying problem while exploring alternative recovery methods. The company has also been testing changes on isolated portions of its infrastructure before applying them more broadly.

微软仍在调查底层故障，同时探索其他恢复方案。公司还在部分隔离的基础设施上测试调整措施，确认有效后再考虑扩大应用范围。

The biggest challenge is ensuring that servers remain stable after being returned to service. Simply restarting them is not enough if they become unresponsive again when normal traffic resumes.

目前最大的挑战，是确保服务器恢复服务后能够持续稳定运行。如果服务器在正常流量恢复后再次失去响应，那么仅仅重启服务器并不能真正解决问题。

As of November 13, Microsoft had not provided a definitive timeline for full recovery.

截至 11 月 13 日，微软尚未公布全部服务恢复正常的明确时间表。

The incident highlights how heavily businesses, governments and everyday users have come to depend on a relatively small number of major cloud service providers. When a critical part of that infrastructure fails, the consequences can spread far beyond a single company.

此次事件凸显了企业、政府和普通用户对少数大型云服务提供商的高度依赖。一旦这些基础设施中的关键环节发生故障，其影响就可能远远超出单一企业的范围。
