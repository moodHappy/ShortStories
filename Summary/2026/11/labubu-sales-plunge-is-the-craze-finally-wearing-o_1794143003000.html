Labubu Sales Plunge: Is the Craze Finally Wearing Off?

发布时间：2026/11/8 21:03:23

销量急挫，Labubu热潮正在退烧吗？

The Labubu craze once seemed unstoppable. Adults and children alike rushed to buy blind boxes containing the quirky little monsters. Each sealed mystery package often cost $15 (108 yuan) or more. Those who failed to get the design they wanted were sometimes willing to pay considerably higher prices on the resale market.

曾几何时，Labubu（拉布布）热潮似乎势不可挡。大人和小孩都争相购买装着这些古怪小怪兽的盲盒，每个密封的神秘包装售价往往在15美元（约合108元人民币）或以上。那些没能抽中心仪款式的人，有时甚至愿意在二手市场支付高得多的价格购买。

The frenzy became so intense that one superfan from North Carolina, known online as Ensito, quit his job in the technology industry in 2024 to create Labubu-related content full-time. He even travelled to Italy and Spain in search of the latest releases.

这股狂热甚至让一位来自美国北卡罗来纳州、网名为 Ensito 的超级粉丝在2024年辞去了科技行业的工作，专职制作 Labubu 相关内容。他还专程前往意大利和西班牙，只为追寻最新发售的产品。

Ensito has spent more than $50,000 (about 360,000 yuan) and amassed a collection of over 400 Labubu figures. He told the BBC that it was a figure he felt almost embarrassed to admit.

截至目前，Ensito 已经花费超过5万美元（约合36万元人民币），收藏了400多个 Labubu。他对 BBC 记者说，这笔开销大到让他“说出来都觉得不好意思”。

He even commissioned a dentist to make a set of custom dentures modelled on Labubu's distinctive jagged teeth, which he wears to fan events.

他甚至专门请牙医定制了一副假牙，仿照 Labubu 标志性的锯齿状牙齿设计，供自己参加粉丝活动时佩戴。

But in recent months, the videos that once made Ensito an online sensation have been attracting noticeably fewer views.

然而，近几个月来，Ensito 过去那些曾让他在网络上走红的视频，播放量明显下滑。

"Those videos used to hit 10,000 views very quickly, but now I'm struggling even to reach that," he said.

他说：“以前那些视频很快就能突破一万次观看，但现在我连做到这一点都很困难。”

Ensito has since shifted his attention to other trending products, including Hello Kitty and Squishies — soft toys that slowly return to their original shape after being squeezed.

如今，Ensito 已将注意力转向其他潮流产品，包括 Hello Kitty（凯蒂猫）和 Squishies（捏捏乐）——这是一种柔软的玩具，被按压后会缓慢恢复原状。

His experience reflects a broader possibility: the Labubu craze may be losing momentum. At its peak, the phenomenon helped propel the market value of its parent company, Pop Mart, beyond the combined value of US toy giants Hasbro and Mattel.

他的经历反映出一种更广泛的趋势：Labubu 热潮可能正在失去动力。在风头最盛时，这股风潮甚至推动其母公司泡泡玛特的市值超过美国玩具巨头孩之宝（Hasbro）和美泰（Mattel）的市值总和。

Analysts say the Beijing-based company is struggling to turn a short-lived viral sensation into lasting consumer appeal.

分析人士认为，这家总部位于北京的公司正面临一个难题：如何将短暂的网络爆红转化为持久的消费者吸引力。

Pop Mart recently reported that global sales for the three months ending in September had fallen by as much as 50% year on year.

泡泡玛特近日公布的数据显示，截至9月底的三个月内，其全球销售额同比下降幅度最高达到50%。

The decline was particularly pronounced outside mainland China, with revenue in the United States falling by at least 70%.

中国大陆以外市场的跌幅尤其明显，其中美国市场收入至少下降了70%。

## The Rise and Fall of a Collectible Craze

## 潮起潮落：一个收藏品热潮的兴衰

Pop Mart did not invent the blind-box concept, but its figures undoubtedly helped popularise the sales model around the world.

泡泡玛特并不是盲盒销售模式的发明者，但毫无疑问，旗下潮玩公仔推动了这种销售模式在全球范围内的普及。

Katelynn Carlson, who works in marketing, says part of the appeal of blind boxes lies in the nostalgic feeling they evoke — much like the excitement of opening presents as a child.

从事市场营销工作的凯特琳·卡尔森（Katelynn Carlson）表示，盲盒的吸引力之一，在于它能唤起人们童年拆礼物时的怀旧感和期待感。

Carlson discovered Labubu through social media and has since collected 36 figures. She said one of her early purchases turned out to be a rare design, making the experience even more exciting.

卡尔森是通过社交媒体认识 Labubu 的，如今已经收藏了36个。她表示，自己早期购买的其中一个公仔恰好是稀有款，这让整个收藏过程变得更加刺激。

Launched in 2019, Labubu has become Pop Mart's most successful toy series. The creatures eventually evolved into fashion accessories and status symbols, with celebrities such as Kim Kardashian and Lisa, a member of the South Korean girl group Blackpink, publicly showing off their collections.

Labubu 于2019年推出，后来成为泡泡玛特最成功的玩具系列。这些小怪兽逐渐演变成时尚配饰和潮流身份象征。金·卡戴珊（Kim Kardashian）以及韩国女团 Blackpink 成员 Lisa 等名人，都曾公开展示相关产品。

Labubu releases have regularly drawn huge queues of customers. The frenzy became so intense that Pop Mart temporarily removed the toys from its UK stores after customers began competing aggressively to get their hands on them. One collector reportedly paid more than $150,000 for a life-sized Labubu.

Labubu 每次推出新品，经常都会吸引大批顾客排队抢购。由于顾客争抢过于激烈，泡泡玛特一度暂时将相关玩具从英国门店下架。据报道，还有收藏家花费超过15万美元购买了一个真人大小的 Labubu。

Dr Samer El Hajjar, a marketing lecturer at the National University of Singapore, said one of Labubu's main attractions was the difficulty of obtaining certain designs. Fans often bought blind boxes repeatedly in the hope of getting the figure they wanted, further fuelling the craze.

新加坡国立大学市场学讲师萨默尔·埃尔-哈贾尔博士（Samer El Hajjar）表示，Labubu 的主要吸引力之一，在于某些款式极难入手。为了抽中心仪的公仔，粉丝往往会反复购买盲盒，这又进一步推高了市场热度。

Pop Mart currently operates around 500 stores in more than 30 countries, alongside approximately 2,000 vending machines worldwide. Listed in Hong Kong in 2020, the company is still valued at close to $24bn. Despite its share price falling by more than 50% from last year's peak, it remains one of the world's most valuable toy companies.

泡泡玛特目前在30多个国家拥有约500家门店，并在全球运营约2,000台自动贩卖机。这家于2020年在香港上市的公司，目前市值仍接近240亿美元。尽管其股价较去年高点已经下跌超过50%，但它依然是全球市值最高的玩具企业之一。

El Hajjar said investors were concerned that Pop Mart's global success might turn out to be a viral phenomenon rather than the result of sustainable growth driven by long-term customer loyalty.

埃尔-哈贾尔表示，投资者担心，泡泡玛特在全球取得的成功可能只是一次网络爆红现象，而不是建立在长期顾客忠诚度基础上的可持续增长。

For Ensito, the fading sense of scarcity is clearly part of the problem.

对 Ensito 来说，稀缺感的消失显然是问题的一部分。

"That feeling just isn't there anymore. I walk into a Pop Mart store and the entire Labubu collection is sitting there," he said.

他说：“现在已经没有那种感觉了。我走进一家泡泡玛特门店，发现整个 Labubu 系列都摆在那里。”

Ashley Dudarenok, founder of China-based market research firm ChoZan, said Pop Mart had significantly increased production to meet demand, gradually eroding the sense of exclusivity and rarity that had helped make the toys so desirable.

中国市场研究公司超赞（ChoZan）创办人艾熙丽（Ashley Dudarenok）表示，为了满足市场需求，泡泡玛特大幅提高了产量，而这逐渐削弱了产品原本独特、稀有的感觉。

Pop Mart declined to comment on its share-price performance and did not respond to questions about the reported sales decline.

泡泡玛特拒绝就股价表现发表评论，也没有回应有关销售额下滑的提问。

## Rivals Enter the Blind-Box Market

## 竞争对手纷纷进军盲盒市场

As the Labubu craze cools, some of its competitors are becoming increasingly popular.

随着 Labubu 热潮逐渐降温，一些竞争对手的产品反而越来越受欢迎。

Blind-box collectibles such as Sonny Angel — small, angel-like figures that are typically depicted naked — and glow-in-the-dark Smiski miniatures have continued to sell well. Even established franchises such as My Little Pony and KPop Demon Hunters have entered the blind-box market.

Sonny Angel——一种通常以赤身裸体形象呈现、外形宛如小天使的迷你玩偶——以及能够在黑暗中发光的 Smiski 小公仔等盲盒收藏品，近期仍然持续热销。如今，就连《小马宝莉》和《KPop 猎魔女团》等已有知名度的品牌，也纷纷推出了盲盒产品。

Miniso, the Guangzhou-based retailer, now sells blind-box collections featuring Disney and Marvel characters across more than 8,000 stores worldwide.

总部位于中国广州的零售商名创优品，目前已在全球超过8,000家门店销售迪士尼和漫威角色系列盲盒。

Vincent Huang, a Miniso executive, said earlier this year: "Consumers don't really care that much about where a brand comes from."

名创优品管理人员黄铮（Vincent Huang）今年早些时候表示：“消费者其实并不特别在意一个品牌来自哪里。”

"They care more about the shopping experience — the design, value for money and the fun of buying."

“他们更关心的是购物体验，比如设计、性价比，以及购买过程中的乐趣。”

Experts say one of Pop Mart's biggest challenges is building characters that can remain popular over the long term.

专家指出，泡泡玛特面临的最大挑战之一，是打造能够长期保持人气、经久不衰的角色形象。

Analyst Boustani said Sanrio, the company behind Hello Kitty, had a clear advantage in this respect. Sanrio reported record results for the financial year ending in March.

分析师布斯塔尼（Boustani）认为，在这方面，Hello Kitty 的母公司三丽鸥（Sanrio）显然更具优势。三丽鸥在截至今年3月的财年中创下了业绩纪录。

Some experts believe Pop Mart has invested heavily in expanding its brand beyond toys. Its recent efforts include opening a theme park in Beijing and announcing plans for a Labubu film, moves resembling the strategies used by entertainment and licensing giants such as Disney and Lego.

一些专家认为，泡泡玛特近年来一直在大力投资，试图将品牌影响力从玩具领域扩展出去。其相关举措包括在北京开设主题乐园，以及宣布计划拍摄 Labubu 电影。这些做法与迪士尼、乐高等娱乐和品牌授权巨头的经营策略有相似之处。

But Dudarenok said Pop Mart was taking the opposite route to Disney. Disney typically builds characters through stories before turning them into merchandise, whereas Pop Mart has largely built its characters through toys first.

但艾熙丽认为，泡泡玛特走的恰恰是与迪士尼相反的路线。迪士尼通常先通过故事塑造角色，再将角色转化为商品；而泡泡玛特在很大程度上是先销售玩具，再逐步塑造角色的影响力。

"Next year will be the real test of whether Pop Mart can make people fall in love with a character without the help of celebrities," she said.

她说：“明年真正的考验，是泡泡玛特能否在没有名人加持的情况下，仍然让人们爱上一个角色。”

## "I Only Buy What I Like Now"

## “现在我只买自己真正喜欢的”

After spending so much money collecting the little monsters, Ensito said he had begun to reflect on how much time and money people devote to collectibles whenever a new craze emerges.

在花费巨额金钱收藏这些小怪兽之后，Ensito 表示，每当一种新的潮流出现，他如今都会开始反思，人们究竟为这些收藏品投入了多少时间和金钱。

"Every year there's a new craze. There's always something that suddenly becomes incredibly popular," he said.

他说：“每年都会出现一股新的潮流，总会有某样东西突然变得非常受欢迎。”

Lawrence Yu, a content creator from Melbourne, said that after spending two years chasing Labubu, he would probably become "more selective" if he decided to add to his collection of around 50 figures.

来自墨尔本的内容创作者劳伦斯·余（Lawrence Yu）表示，在追逐 Labubu 两年之后，如果未来还要为自己约50个公仔的收藏增添新成员，他很可能会变得“更加挑剔”。

His Labubu purchasing and unboxing videos once attracted as many as one million views. But as the craze has faded, he now rarely produces content about the toys.

他过去发布的 Labubu 购买及开箱视频，播放量曾高达100万次。但随着热潮消退，他如今已经很少再制作相关内容。

"I do think the craze has slowed down. People go crazy for something for a while, and then the excitement gradually fades," he said.

他说：“我确实认为这股热潮已经放缓了……人们会在一段时间内疯狂追捧某样东西，然后热情就会慢慢消退。”

Clara Barbero, from Canada, believes Labubu has become a "dead trend", although she still keeps an eye out for new releases that might be worth collecting.

来自加拿大的克拉拉·巴贝罗（Clara Barbero）认为，Labubu 已经成为一种“过气潮流”，不过她仍然会留意是否有值得收藏的新产品推出。

"I am now more intentional about curating my collection. I only buy designs that I think I will still love in the years to come, rather than buying something every time a new series comes out," she said.

她说：“现在我会更有意识地整理自己的收藏。我只购买那些我认为未来几年仍然会喜欢的款式，而不是每次有新系列推出就买。”

But Labubu still has a deeply loyal fan base.

不过，Labubu 仍然拥有一批十分忠实的粉丝。

Hedy Khaled, from Liverpool, said that although the craze surrounding the figures was cooling, she still loved her collection of 70 Pop Mart figures just as much as before.

来自利物浦的海蒂·哈立德（Hedy Khaled）表示，尽管这些公仔的热潮正在降温，但她对自己收藏的70个泡泡玛特公仔依然一如既往地喜爱。

She believes there is a clear distinction between people who chase trends and "real fans", arguing that falling sales do not necessarily mean Labubu has lost its appeal.

她认为，追逐潮流的人与“真正的粉丝”之间存在明显区别，因此，销售额下滑并不一定意味着 Labubu 已经失去了吸引力。

For Carlson, the reason she continues collecting is not the temporary excitement of an online trend, but the happiness the figures bring her. She remains excited by new Pop Mart releases, including a recently launched cat-themed Labubu collection that has become one of her favourites.

对卡尔森而言，让她继续收藏的原因并不是一时的网络热潮，而是这些公仔带给她的快乐。泡泡玛特每次推出新品，依然会让她感到兴奋。比如近期推出的猫咪主题 Labubu 系列，就已经成为她最喜欢的系列之一。

"I think there's something special about embracing your inner child and simply liking something because it makes you happy," she said.

她说：“我认为，拥抱内心那个孩子般的自己，仅仅因为某样东西能让自己快乐就去喜欢它，这本身就有一种特别的意义。”

The Labubu story may therefore be entering a new phase. The extraordinary demand driven by scarcity, celebrity exposure and social-media attention may be fading, but that does not necessarily mean the character itself is finished. The more difficult test for Pop Mart is whether it can turn occasional buyers into long-term collectors — and build a brand that remains attractive after the next viral sensation arrives.

因此，Labubu 的故事或许正在进入一个新阶段。由稀缺性、名人效应和社交媒体关注共同推动的惊人需求，可能正在减弱，但这并不意味着这个角色本身已经走向终结。对泡泡玛特而言，真正困难的考验是：能否将一时冲动的消费者转变为长期收藏者，并建立一个即使在下一股网络爆红潮流出现之后，依然具有吸引力的品牌。
