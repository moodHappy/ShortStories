Once Put on the Scales, a Thousand Pounds Won't Be Enough: Why Jensen Huang Became a Casualty of Washington Politics

发布时间：2026/11/14 12:12:21

一上秤，一千斤也打不住：黄仁勋为何成了华盛顿的牺牲品？

There is a Chinese saying that seems particularly fitting for what has happened to Jensen Huang: "Some things weigh less than a few ounces until you put them on the scales. Then, suddenly, they can weigh a thousand pounds." Until recently, Huang appeared capable of navigating Washington's political turbulence with considerable ease. Thanks to his close relationship with Donald Trump, he could take his business concerns directly to the president and, at least for a time, influence the direction of US policy on chip exports to China. Yet this seemingly solid political protection overlooked one fundamental reality: the American president is not the entirety of the American political machinery.

中国有句俗话，用来形容黄仁勋这次的遭遇，再合适不过：「有些事不上秤没有四两重，一上秤一千斤也打不住。」此前，他似乎一直能够在华盛顿的政治风浪中游刃有余。凭借与特朗普建立的密切关系，他不仅能够直接向总统表达自己的商业诉求，甚至一度能够影响美国政府对华芯片出口政策的走向。然而，这种政治庇护看似牢固，却忽略了一个问题：美国总统并不是美国政治机器的全部。

Huang clearly had confidence in his ability to persuade. He could explain the commercial value of the Chinese market to Trump, argue that American companies should not voluntarily surrender such an enormous market, and repeatedly make the case that selling compliant chips to China would help preserve the global competitiveness of US businesses. These arguments were not without merit from a commercial perspective, and Trump appeared willing to entertain them.

黄仁勋显然相信自己的说服能力。他能够向特朗普解释中国市场的商业价值，强调美国企业不应该主动放弃庞大的市场，更反复论证继续向中国出售符合出口规定的芯片，有助于维护美国企业的全球竞争力。这些论点在商业层面并非没有道理，而特朗普也曾表现出愿意听取这套逻辑的态度。

But Huang appears to have underestimated another force: the China hawks in Washington.

但黄仁勋似乎低估了另一股力量：华盛顿的对华鹰派。

He may have been able to persuade Trump, but that does not mean he could persuade lawmakers, national security officials and policy hardliners who have long advocated technological restrictions on China. For these figures, the commercial value of the Chinese market is not the primary consideration. Their focus is whether China can gain access to advanced computing capabilities and whether the United States should allow a strategic competitor to obtain such technology through ordinary commercial channels.

他或许能够说服特朗普，却未必能够说服那些长期主张对中国实施技术封锁的国会议员、国家安全官员和政策强硬派。对于这些人而言，中国市场的商业价值并不是最重要的考量。他们关注的是中国能否获得先进计算能力，以及美国是否应该允许一个战略竞争对手通过正常商业渠道获取这些技术。

This created a rather delicate situation: Trump could accept Huang's commercial reasoning, but other parts of the American political system had no intention of following suit. Presidential support could change executive policy, but it could not necessarily eliminate congressional opposition, let alone persuade the entire national security establishment to abandon its own strategic calculations.

这就形成了一种颇为微妙的局面：特朗普可以接受黄仁勋的商业逻辑，但美国政治体系中的另一部分力量并不打算接受。总统的支持可以改变行政部门的政策，却未必能够消除国会的反对，更不可能让整个国家安全体系放弃自身的战略判断。

More importantly, the 2026 midterm elections brought a new political landscape to Washington. Republicans suffered major setbacks, while Democrats won majorities in both chambers of Congress. Trump lost the advantage of unified Republican control of Congress, and existing divisions within his own party now face a new struggle over political influence.

更值得注意的是，2026年中期选举给华盛顿带来了新的政治格局。共和党遭遇重大挫败，民主党赢得参众两院多数。特朗普失去了国会两院的统一执政优势，原本就存在的党内分歧也面临新的权力调整。

This does not mean Trump has lost all control over the Republican Party. But it does suggest that his political authority can no longer be translated as easily into unified action across the party and Congress. For Republican hawks who have never fully embraced his approach to China, this creates additional political room to manoeuvre.

这并不意味着特朗普已经失去了对共和党的所有控制力，但至少说明，他的政治权威不再能够像此前那样轻易转化为整个共和党和国会的统一行动。对于那些本来就不认同他对华政策的共和党鹰派而言，这无疑提供了更多政治空间。

At the same time, Democrats have little political incentive to surrender ground on China policy. A hard line towards Beijing has long been an important area of overlap between the two parties. Republicans and Democrats may fight bitterly over taxes, immigration, healthcare, energy and even the president himself, yet they can still find common ground when it comes to restricting China's access to advanced technology.

与此同时，民主党也没有理由在对华问题上主动让出政治立场。对华强硬早已成为美国两党政治中的重要交集。两党可以在税收、移民、医疗、能源乃至总统个人问题上激烈争斗，却可能在限制中国获取先进技术的问题上找到共同语言。

Huang was therefore no longer dealing merely with Trump's personal position. He was facing a political alignment that crossed party lines. Republican hawks wanted to demonstrate their toughness on national security, while Democratic hawks had little desire to give Republicans an opportunity to accuse them of being soft on China. Their political interests converged.

于是，黄仁勋面对的就不再只是特朗普一个人的态度，而是一个跨越党派的政治联盟。共和党鹰派希望证明自己在国家安全问题上立场坚定，民主党鹰派也不愿被共和党指责对中国软弱。双方的政治利益在这里发生了交汇。

From this perspective, national security is both a genuine policy concern and an important language through which competing political factions seek to gain the upper hand.

从这个角度看，所谓国家安全，既是一个真实存在的政策问题，也成为了各方争夺政治主动权的重要语言。

Of course, the United States has every right to assess the risks associated with exporting sensitive technology. If evidence shows that individuals were involved in espionage, violated export controls or facilitated the illegal diversion of chips, investigations and accountability are entirely justified. No business leader should be exempt from the law because of commercial success.

当然，美国有权审查敏感技术的出口风险。如果有证据表明相关人员涉嫌从事间谍活动、违反出口管制规定，或者存在芯片非法转移，那么调查和追责完全合理。任何企业家都不能因为商业成就而获得法律豁免。

But where exactly are the boundaries of national security?

但问题在于，国家安全的边界究竟在哪里？

Does a controversy involving personal relationships provide sufficient grounds for tightening export restrictions across an entire industry? Does maintaining commercial ties with the Chinese market automatically make an entrepreneur a national security risk? If the government believes that specific risks exist, it should present specific evidence, rather than automatically placing all business activities involving China under suspicion.

一个涉及个人关系的争议，是否足以成为全面收紧一个行业出口政策的理由？一个企业家与中国市场保持商业联系，是否就意味着他对国家安全构成了威胁？如果政府认为存在具体风险，就应该拿出具体证据，而不是让所有涉及中国的商业活动都自动进入怀疑名单。

What is particularly noteworthy is that recent congressional developments have moved well beyond investigating Huang personally. Lawmakers from both parties are pushing for tighter chip export restrictions and seeking broader oversight of chip destinations, overseas data centres and related supply chains. In other words, even Huang's departure from Nvidia's leadership will not bring this policy battle to an end.

尤其值得注意的是，最近的国会动向已经不再局限于调查黄仁勋个人。两党议员开始推动更严格的芯片出口限制，并试图扩大对芯片流向、海外数据中心和相关供应链的监管。换句话说，即使黄仁勋离开英伟达的领导岗位，这场政策斗争也不会因此结束。

Huang's greatest mistake may not have been his emphasis on the Chinese market, but his apparent belief that a personal relationship with the president could provide lasting protection on an issue that had already become deeply politicised. He placed considerable faith in Trump's support while underestimating the convergence of interests among Congress, intelligence agencies and hawks in both parties.

黄仁勋可能犯下的最大错误，并不是重视中国市场，而是过于相信自己能够凭借与总统的私人关系，在一个已经高度政治化的议题上获得长期保护。他把特朗普的支持看得太重，却低估了国会、情报机构和两党鹰派之间的利益交汇。

Perhaps this is the most revealing aspect of the entire controversy: an entrepreneur capable of influencing the global technology industry may still be unable to shape the political currents in Washington. He can talk about markets, jobs and the competitiveness of American companies. But when these commercial arguments collide with the national security narrative shared by both parties, his personal influence can prove remarkably limited.

这也许正是这场风波最值得玩味的地方：一个能够影响全球科技产业的企业家，最终却未必能够左右华盛顿的政治风向。他可以谈市场、谈就业、谈美国企业的竞争力，但当这些商业逻辑与两党共同的国家安全叙事发生冲突时，个人影响力就显得格外有限。

Huang's predicament also reveals an uncomfortable reality about American politics: a president may open a door for a business leader, but he cannot guarantee that Congress, the bureaucracy or political rivals will not close it again. When an issue touches on party competition, national security and America's strategy towards China all at once, even the most valuable personal connections can become fragile.

黄仁勋的遭遇，也让人看到美国政治体系中的一种现实：总统可以给企业家打开一扇门，但无法保证这扇门永远不会被国会、官僚体系和政治对手重新关上。尤其当一个问题同时触及党派竞争、国家安全和对华战略时，任何个人关系都可能变得不堪一击。

Ultimately, Huang is neither the centre of American politics nor the decisive figure in the broader US-China rivalry. He simply found himself standing at the intersection of several powerful political forces. When Trump's political protection proved insufficient to withstand congressional pressure, and hawks in both parties found common ground on China, Huang went from being a business leader with direct access to the president to a focal point of Washington's political struggle.

说到底，黄仁勋不是美国政治的中心，也不是这场中美博弈的决定性人物。他只是恰好站在了几股政治力量交汇的地方。当特朗普的政治庇护不足以抵挡国会的压力，当共和党和民主党鹰派在对华问题上找到共同利益，他便从一个能够与总统直接对话的商业领袖，变成了各方政治角力中的具体对象。

When weighed separately, personal connections, commercial interests and presidential promises may all seem substantial. But once placed on the scales of the entire American political system, an individual's influence may count for very little. Huang may now have learned a difficult lesson about Washington: what truly carries weight is not merely a president's promise, but the consensus that the political machinery as a whole can build around an issue.

不上秤的时候，个人关系、商业利益和总统承诺似乎都很有分量；一旦放到美国整个政治体系的秤盘上，个人的分量就未必还剩多少。黄仁勋这次恐怕终于明白：在华盛顿，真正有分量的从来不只是总统的承诺，还有整个政治机器在某个问题上形成的共识。
