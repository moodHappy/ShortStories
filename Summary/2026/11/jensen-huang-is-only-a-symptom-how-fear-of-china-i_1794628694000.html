Jensen Huang Is Only a Symptom: How Fear of China Is Gripping Washington

发布时间：2026/11/14 11:58:14

黄仁勋只是一个缩影：美国政界正在被对华恐惧裹挟

The controversy surrounding Jensen Huang should not be viewed merely as a business dispute, or even simply as a disagreement over technology policy. It is more like a mirror reflecting an increasingly visible tendency in American politics: suspicion of China is expanding and gradually permeating almost every aspect of the country's political, economic and social landscape.

黄仁勋所遭遇的风波，或许不应该仅仅被理解为一场商业纠纷，甚至不应该仅仅被理解为一场科技政策之争。它更像是一面镜子，映照出当下美国政界一种日益明显的政治倾向：对中国的警惕正在不断扩大，并逐渐渗透到美国政治、经济和社会议题的各个角落。

Huang is merely one part of a much larger picture.

黄仁勋只是这幅图景中的一个缩影。

For much of the past, American policy towards China, despite its persistent tensions and disagreements, still allowed room for different approaches. Some emphasised economic cooperation, others advocated strategic competition, while some sought a balance between the two. Even amid intense debate, China was not necessarily at the centre of every major domestic political issue in the United States.

过去，美国对华政策虽然始终存在竞争与分歧，但不同政治力量之间仍然保留着一定的政策空间。有人强调经济合作，有人主张战略竞争，也有人认为应该在竞争与合作之间寻找平衡。即使存在激烈争论，中国也并非美国所有国内政治问题的中心。

But that appears to be changing.

但今天，情况正在发生变化。

Taking a hard line on China is increasingly becoming a politically convenient position. Whether in Congress, the executive branch or on the campaign trail, politicians often find it easier to demonstrate their toughness towards Beijing than to explain why some room for cooperation should be preserved.

对华强硬越来越容易成为一种政治正确。无论是国会中的议员、行政部门的官员，还是竞选中的政治人物，展示自己对中国的强硬立场，往往比解释为什么需要保留合作空间更容易获得政治支持。

This trend extends far beyond semiconductors and artificial intelligence.

这种趋势并不局限于芯片和人工智能。

From trade and investment to manufacturing, education, scientific research and social media; from supply chains and infrastructure to national security, an increasing number of issues that could once have been discussed on their own are now being placed within the framework of US-China strategic competition. A company's commercial decision can become a national security issue. An ordinary academic exchange can invite political scrutiny. Even a routine market transaction can acquire geopolitical significance.

从贸易、投资、制造业，到教育、科研、社交媒体，再到供应链、基础设施和国家安全，越来越多原本可以独立讨论的问题，都被放进了中美战略竞争的框架之中。一个企业的商业选择，可以被上升到国家安全；一次正常的学术交流，可以引发政治审视；一项普通的市场交易，也可能被赋予地缘政治意义。

The issue is not that the United States should never be wary of China, nor that China's development cannot create competition and friction. Conflicts of interest are inevitable between major powers, and national security is a legitimate concern that deserves serious attention.

问题并不是美国不能警惕中国，也不是中国的发展不可能带来竞争和摩擦。任何大国之间都存在利益冲突，国家安全也确实需要认真对待。

What deserves greater scrutiny is what happens when suspicion towards another country gradually becomes a political assumption that no longer needs to be demonstrated. At that point, rational debate begins to lose its space.

真正值得警惕的是，当对一个国家的怀疑逐渐变成一种不需要证明的政治前提时，理性讨论就会失去空间。

In such an environment, politicians no longer need to carefully distinguish between specific threats and abstract competition. Nor must they always explain what a particular restriction is actually intended to achieve. Once a policy is linked to China, it can appear to acquire a degree of legitimacy almost automatically.

在这种环境下，政治人物不再需要仔细区分具体威胁与抽象竞争，也不必总是解释某项限制措施究竟能够解决什么问题。只要将其与中国联系起来，政策本身似乎就获得了某种天然的正当性。

This is what makes the Huang controversy particularly significant.

这也是黄仁勋事件值得关注的地方。

Huang is not a foreign political figure opposing American national interests. He is an entrepreneur who built his career in the United States and leads a global company that has generated enormous commercial value for the American technology industry. His emphasis on the importance of the Chinese market and his efforts to preserve Nvidia's business presence there represent a commercial position that should be open to public debate and regulatory scrutiny.

黄仁勋并不是一个反对美国国家利益的外国政治人物，而是一位在美国成长、经营全球企业、为美国科技产业创造巨大商业价值的企业家。他强调中国市场的重要性，试图维持英伟达在中国的商业空间。这本来是一种可以公开讨论、可以接受政策审查的商业立场。

Yet when such a position is increasingly viewed through the lens of national loyalty, security risks and political allegiance, the question becomes much larger than how a company should conduct its business. It becomes a question of whether American society still allows business leaders to disagree with Washington's approach to China.

然而，当这种立场越来越容易被放进国家忠诚、安全风险乃至政治立场的框架中时，问题就不再只是企业应该如何做生意，而是美国社会是否还允许企业家对华盛顿的对华政策提出不同意见。

Of course, specific allegations must be investigated on the basis of evidence. Huang should not receive special treatment simply because he is an American business leader, nor should every criticism be dismissed as political persecution simply because China is involved. But invoking national security should not exempt political motives, policy boundaries or practical consequences from scrutiny either.

当然，具体指控应当依据证据调查，不能因为黄仁勋是美国企业家就给予特殊待遇，也不能因为事件涉及中国就预设所有质疑都是政治迫害。但同样不能因为打着国家安全的旗号，就放弃对政治动机、政策边界和实际后果的审视。

The broader problem is that this political climate is beginning to reinforce itself.

更大的问题在于，这种政治气候正在形成自我强化的循环。

The more politicians emphasise the China threat, the easier it becomes to appeal to voters and political groups that favour a hard line towards Beijing. The tougher American policy becomes, the more likely economic and diplomatic relations are to deteriorate. And the more strained those relations become, the easier it is for new disputes to be presented as further evidence of the China threat.

政治人物越是强调中国威胁，就越容易获得支持对华强硬的选民和政治团体的认同；对华政策越是强硬，双方的经济与外交关系就越容易恶化；关系越是紧张，新的摩擦又越容易被解释为中国威胁的证据。

Within this cycle, those who advocate de-escalation may be accused of weakness, those who favour cooperation may be dismissed as naïve, and those seeking to protect commercial interests may find themselves under political scrutiny.

在这样的循环中，缓和关系的人可能被质疑软弱，主张合作的人可能被怀疑天真，而试图维护商业利益的人甚至可能被置于政治审视之下。

This does not mean that America has lost its internal policy disagreements. Different interests, voices and strategic calculations continue to exist within its political system. What is noteworthy, however, is that even amid these disagreements, taking a hard line on China is increasingly becoming a common political language through which competing factions seek to gain the upper hand.

这并不意味着美国已经失去了内部的政策分歧。事实上，美国政治体系内部仍然存在不同的利益、声音和战略判断。但值得注意的是，即使存在分歧，对华强硬也越来越容易成为各方争夺政治主动权时共同使用的语言。

And that is perhaps the most important point to consider: when domestic political competition increasingly relies on the construction of external threats, a foreign rival may cease to be merely a country that needs to be studied and dealt with seriously. It can gradually become a character that domestic politics itself requires.

而这恰恰是最值得深思的地方：当一个国家的政治竞争越来越依赖塑造外部威胁时，外部竞争对手就可能不再只是一个需要认真研究和应对的对象，而逐渐成为国内政治运转所需要的角色。

Huang's predicament is only one manifestation of this broader trend. Today it may be a semiconductor company; tomorrow it could be a university, a research institution, a multinational corporation, or any individual or organisation seeking to maintain normal relations across the US-China divide.

黄仁勋的遭遇只是其中一个面向。今天是芯片企业，明天可能是大学、科研机构、跨国公司，或者任何一个试图在中美之间维持正常往来的个人与组织。

The United States has every right to protect its national interests and compete with China. But a country genuinely confident in its own position should be capable of distinguishing competition from hostility, genuine risks from speculation, and national security from political theatre. Fear of a competitor should not become the sole standard by which it judges its own businesses, academic institutions and social interactions.

美国当然有权保护自己的国家利益，也有权与中国展开竞争。但一个真正有信心的国家，应当能够区分竞争与敌意、风险与臆测、国家安全与政治表演。它不应该把对竞争对手的恐惧，变成审视本国企业、学术机构和社会交往的唯一尺度。

Perhaps the real question facing America is not how to silence Jensen Huang, nor how to further restrict China's technological space. It is something more fundamental: why is a country with the world's leading universities, corporations, capital markets and innovative capabilities becoming increasingly uncomfortable with the existence of differing views on China within its own political and business communities?

美国真正需要回答的，或许不是如何让黄仁勋闭嘴，也不是如何进一步压缩中国的技术空间，而是一个更根本的问题：一个拥有世界顶尖大学、企业、资本和创新能力的国家，为什么越来越难以容忍自己的政治和商业体系中存在不同的对华声音？

A nation's strength should not rest on an endless fear of another. If China's rise has become an all-purpose framework through which American politicians explain domestic problems, mobilise political support and scrutinise their own society, then perhaps it is no longer China alone that Washington needs to examine.

一个国家的强大，不应该建立在对另一个国家永无止境的恐惧之上。倘若中国的发展已经成为美国政界解释自身问题、动员政治支持乃至审视本国社会的万能框架，那么美国需要反思的，恐怕早已不只是中国。
