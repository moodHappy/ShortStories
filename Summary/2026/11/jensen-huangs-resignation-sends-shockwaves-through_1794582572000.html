Jensen Huang’s Resignation Sends Shockwaves Through China: ‘Chinese Spy’ Memes, Election Conspiracies and a $600 Billion Stock Market Nightmare

发布时间：2026/11/13 23:09:32

黄仁勋辞职余波：中国舆论场上的“间谍”迷因、选举猜想与股价“黑天鹅”

After a series of extramarital affairs came to light, Nvidia founder Jensen Huang announced on Thursday (November 12) that he was stepping down as chief executive and a member of the company's board. Within a week of the scandal breaking, global media and social media platforms had become consumed by the story, and the online debate shows little sign of dying down.

接连被揭发多段婚外情后，英伟达（Nvidia）创始人黄仁勋于周四（11月12日）宣布辞去行政总裁（CEO）及董事会成员职务。事件曝光后的一周内，全球媒体与社交平台全面卷入，舆论至今仍在持续发酵。

The focus quickly shifted from the affairs themselves to the rumoured women involved, particularly a Chinese American woman identified as Alice Huang. Her identity and background remain unverified, but the phrase “Chinese spy” has become a new internet buzzword.

讨论的焦点很快从婚外情本身转向传闻中的出轨对象，尤其是一名被称为黄乐的华裔女性。关于她的身份与背景，至今没有任何可靠答案，但“Chinese spy”（华人间谍）却由此成为新的网络热词。

In China, the authorities have not officially defined the story or attempted to control the online discussion. The Foreign Ministry dismissed the claims about a “Chinese spy” as baseless smears and malicious accusations. In this unusual space for relatively unrestricted debate, Chinese internet users have focused on gossip, conspiracy theories about the US midterm elections, and the idea that Huang may have been a political casualty.

在中国，官方没有为事件定调，也没有进行舆论管控。外交部仅回应称，所谓“中国间谍”的论调纯属“捕风捉影、污蔑抹黑”。在这个难得的相对自由的讨论空间里，中国网民的兴趣落在八卦、美国中期选举的阴谋论，以及黄仁勋可能成为“政治牺牲品”的叙事上。

The reaction in financial markets has been equally dramatic. On the day Huang's resignation was announced, Nvidia shares plunged by more than 14% at one point during trading, wiping out as much as $600 billion in market value. Investors jokingly described the shock as a “black swan” event.

资本市场的反应同样剧烈。黄仁勋辞职消息公布当日，英伟达股价盘中跌幅一度超过14%，市值最多蒸发约6,000亿美元。股民戏称，这场突如其来的冲击简直就是一只“黑天鹅”。

## The “How to Spot a Chinese Spy” Trend

## “如何辨认中国间谍”的跟风热潮

Huang had earlier admitted to being unfaithful to his wife and issued a public apology. The revelation shocked many because he had long cultivated an image as a devoted husband and family man. In the days that followed, The New York Times and Reuters reported on a second and third alleged affair, while The Economist published four articles examining the consequences of his resignation and the possible candidates to succeed him.

黄仁勋早前承认对妻子不忠，并公开道歉。由于他长期以爱妻、顾家的形象示人，这一消息令舆论哗然。此后数日，《纽约时报》和路透社接连报道他还有第二位、第三位传闻中的出轨对象，《经济学家》更连续刊发四篇文章，讨论他辞职的影响以及可能的继任者。

Who is Alice Huang? What passport does she hold? Did she approach Jensen Huang with a specific mission? None of these questions has a confirmed answer, and no official institution has publicly provided one. Faced with a huge information vacuum, internet users have taken over the conversation.

黄乐究竟是谁？她持有哪国护照？她是否带着某种任务接近黄仁勋？这些问题全部没有答案，也没有任何官方机构出面回应。在巨大的信息空白中，网民接管了舆论场。

From the Chinese internet to Instagram, Threads and TikTok, the discussion has converged on one question: What exactly does a “Chinese spy” look like? Some users have attempted to identify supposed characteristics based on Huang's appearance, comparing her with Wendi Deng, the former wife of media mogul Rupert Murdoch, and Priscilla Chan, the wife of Meta founder Mark Zuckerberg. They have urged business leaders to “beware of China” and warned technology companies to watch out for these so-called “Chinese spies”.

从中国互联网到Instagram、Threads和TikTok，讨论最终归结为一个问题：“中国间谍”究竟是什么样子？有人根据黄乐的外形总结所谓特征，并把她与传媒大亨默多克的前妻邓文迪、Meta创始人马克·扎克伯格的妻子普莉希拉·陈相提并论，呼吁各行业巨头“小心中国”，提醒科技公司提防这些所谓的“华人间谍”。

Online creators were quick to jump on the trend. Fashion influencers posted tutorials on how to “dress like a Chinese spy”; beauty bloggers demonstrated a “Chinese spy makeup look”; relationship influencers published posts declaring, “She's not into you — she's just a Chinese spy.” One podcast host even used a whiteboard to teach viewers how to judge whether their girlfriends might have links to the Chinese government based on their interests.

网络创作者迅速跟上这股风潮：穿搭博主教人如何“穿得像个中国间谍”，美妆博主示范“中国间谍妆”，情感博主写下“她不是喜欢你，她只是个中国间谍”。还有播客主持人在白板上教观众，如何根据女友的兴趣特征推断她是否与中国政府有关。

These posts, whether intended as jokes or taken seriously, have attracted millions of likes. Together, they have created a genuine online phenomenon: how do you recognise a “Chinese spy”?

这些内容无论是玩笑还是认真发布，动辄都能收获上百万点赞。它们共同形成了一股实实在在的网络热潮：究竟该怎样辨认一个“中国间谍”？

## A Joke or Racism? Anxiety and Backlash Among Chinese Communities

## 玩笑还是歧视？华人群体的不安与反弹

One important issue deserves closer examination: the English word “Chinese” does not refer exclusively to citizens of China. It can also encompass people of Chinese descent around the world. This has led many to ask whether the discussion is, in fact, a form of racial and gender discrimination.

一个值得深入探讨的问题是：英文“Chinese”并不只指中国公民，也涵盖世界各地的华人群体。这让不少人开始反问：这样的讨论，难道不是一种种族歧视和性别歧视？

Some Chinese users have pointed out that Asians have made enormous contributions to the technology industry, only to find themselves facing a wave of suspicion and hostility. Even on Douyin, a platform where public opinion is more tightly regulated, some users have written in an almost resigned tone that a major technology company would never allow a person of Chinese descent to remain at the top for long.

一些华人用户指出，亚裔为科技行业作出了巨大贡献，最终迎来的却是一场怀疑与敌意的“围猎”。即使在舆论受到较严格管理的抖音上，也有用户以近乎“认命”的语气写道：一家科技巨头公司怎么可能让一个华裔长期担任最高负责人。

One Threads user expressed disappointment that people were not condemning male CEOs of major technology companies, but instead using the scandal as another opportunity to mock Asian women in the tech industry. “It's so sad,” the user wrote. Others asked: “If Jensen Huang has to resign because of an affair, what about the people on the Epstein list?”

Threads上一名用户表示失望：大家没有去声讨科技巨头公司的男性CEO们，反而借这个机会继续嘲笑科技领域的亚裔女性。“这太可悲了。”还有人追问：“黄仁勋出轨就已经辞职，那么爱泼斯坦名单上的人呢？”

Amid this tug-of-war, a different kind of response is gaining prominence: “Being called a ‘Chinese spy’ makes me deeply uncomfortable.” Such expressions of resistance are increasingly becoming a central part of the debate.

在这样的拉扯中，另一种声音正逐渐成为讨论的中心：“被称为‘Chinese Spy’让我很不舒服。”这类带有反抗意味的发言，正在受到越来越多关注。

## Beijing Keeps Its Distance as Chinese Netizens Watch the Drama

## 北京不设限，网民“看热闹”

The Chinese government has yet to issue an official verdict on the affair. State media have largely followed foreign news reports, focusing on the confirmed developments: Huang's admission of infidelity, his resignation and the timeline of events.

中国官方至今没有为事件定性。官方媒体主要跟随外媒信息源进行事实性报道，内容集中于黄仁勋承认出轨、宣布辞职，以及梳理整个事件的时间线。

In its public response, the Foreign Ministry described the claims about a “Chinese spy” as “baseless speculation and malicious smears”. Asked whether Alice Huang was a Chinese citizen or was currently in Beijing, spokeswoman Mao Ning said on Thursday: “I am not aware of the situation you mentioned.”

在对外表态方面，外交部称所谓“中国间谍”的论调“纯属捕风捉影、污蔑抹黑”。被问及黄乐是否为中国公民、目前是否身在北京时，发言人毛宁周四回应：“我不了解你提到的情况。”

Beyond that, China has imposed no apparent restrictions on discussion of the story. There has been no official media campaign to define the narrative, no visible throttling of traffic, no coordinated guidance of comments and apparently no special censorship keywords.

除此之外，中国没有对事件进行明显的舆论管控：没有官媒统一定调，没有明显限流，没有评论引导，甚至似乎也没有设置专门的敏感词。

For Chinese internet users, this has created a rare space for relatively unrestricted discussion. The story is not considered particularly politically sensitive, while speculation about Alice Huang's identity has largely taken on the character of celebrity gossip. Some people claiming to be her former classmates or colleagues have posted their recollections of her, often ending with the same sentiment: “I never imagined she would become so successful.”

对中国网民来说，这形成了一个罕见的相对自由的讨论空间：事件本身不算特别政治敏感，对黄乐身份的猜测又近乎八卦娱乐。一些自称“黄乐同学”或“黄乐前同事”的人开始发文，回忆自己印象中的黄乐，最后往往落在同一句感叹上：“没想到人家现在这么成功。”

The discussion has also quickly expanded beyond the affair into American politics. The scandal broke just after Republicans, led by Donald Trump, suffered a major defeat in the midterm elections. Some users have questioned whether the timing was simply too convenient: How could senior officials not have known about such a major story? Could Huang have become a casualty of American political manoeuvring?

讨论也很快从婚外情本身延伸到了美国政治。由于事件紧随唐纳德·特朗普领导的共和党在中期选举中惨败，不少人怀疑事情发生的时机“太巧了”：这么大的事，高层怎么可能不知道？黄仁勋会不会成为美国政治斗争的牺牲品？

This theory has gained traction on Douyin and Weibo. One Douyin comment, which received more than 3,000 likes, read: “If you can make a man admit to an affair, it means the affair was the least of his problems.” Many users have expressed regret that Huang spent half his life building Nvidia into a technology giant, only to leave under such circumstances.

这类推论在抖音和微博上颇为流行。抖音上一则获得三千多点赞的评论写道：“能让一个男人承认自己出轨，说明出轨是他最小的问题。”许多人惋惜黄仁勋用半辈子把英伟达打造成科技巨头，最终却以这样的理由离场。

## Nvidia Shares Fall Nearly 18% in Eight Trading Days: Investors Call It a “Black Swan”

## 八个交易日股价跌近两成：股民称之为“黑天鹅”

Before the scandal broke, Nvidia shares were still trading at relatively high levels. As the controversy escalated, the stock fell from $202.06 on November 2 to close at $166.19 on November 12. Over eight trading sessions, it lost $35.87, a decline of 17.75%.

事件曝光前，英伟达股价仍处于相对高位。随着丑闻持续发酵，股价从11月2日的202.06美元跌至11月12日收盘时的166.19美元。八个交易日累计下跌35.87美元，跌幅达17.75%。

On November 12, news of Huang's resignation as CEO and a board member triggered an even sharper sell-off after the US market opened. Nvidia shares briefly fell by more than 14%, touching an intraday low of $148.80. At one point, the company's market value had fallen by as much as $600 billion.

11月12日，黄仁勋宣布辞去CEO及董事会成员职务的消息传出后，美股开市不久，英伟达股价遭遇更猛烈的抛售，跌幅一度超过14%，盘中最低触及148.80美元。公司市值一度蒸发高达6,000亿美元。

The shock quickly spread to China's capital markets. On November 13, all three major A-share indices fell, with the ChiNext Index dropping by more than 3%. Semiconductor, AI hardware and optical-module stocks — sectors closely tied to Nvidia's supply chain — were among the hardest hit. Leading suppliers such as TFC Communication and Victory Giant Technology suffered steep losses.

冲击迅速蔓延至中国资本市场。11月13日，A股三大指数全线调整，创业板指下跌超过3%。半导体、AI硬件和光模块等与英伟达产业链密切相关的板块成为重灾区，天孚通信、胜宏科技等供应链龙头纷纷大幅下跌。

Investors are worried about more than just a change of leadership. They are also concerned about Nvidia's future product schedule and supply-chain strategy, as well as whether the United States might use the opportunity to tighten restrictions on AI chips and remote computing services for China. Reuters has reported that several Chinese AI companies have rushed to rent Nvidia computing capacity in overseas data centres, fearing that Washington could close loopholes allowing access to computing power through cloud services.

市场担忧的并不只是英伟达换帅，还包括公司未来的产品节奏与供应链策略，以及美国会否借机收紧对华AI芯片和远程算力限制。据路透社报道，已有多家中国人工智能企业抢租海外数据中心的英伟达算力，以防美国堵住通过云服务获取算力的漏洞。

At the same time, the market has shown a clear split. Funds betting on China's technological self-reliance have flowed into domestic chipmakers. Cambricon rose 8.11% on November 12 and gained more than 5% again in early trading on November 13. Domestic GPU makers Moore Threads and MetaX also recorded gains of more than 8%. As money moved between winners and losers, the Shanghai Composite and CSI 300 indices were broadly flat before midday.

与此同时，市场也出现了明显分化。押注“科技自主”的资金涌入国产芯片股。寒武纪在11月12日大涨8.11%，13日早盘再度上涨超过5%；摩尔线程与沐曦（MetaX）等国产GPU企业涨幅也均超过8%。多空资金此消彼长，上证综指与沪深300指数在午市前基本持平。

On financial forums and stock-market discussion boards, investors have jokingly dubbed the sudden plunge a “black swan”. With Nvidia's succession plans still unresolved, the impact of this unexpected shock is clearly far from over.

在财经论坛和股吧里，股民把这场毫无征兆的暴跌戏称为“黑天鹅”。而随着英伟达继任人选仍悬而未决，这只“黑天鹅”带来的影响显然还远未结束。
